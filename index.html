Advancing Knowledge.

GARRF is an education and research-oriented foundation established to encourage scientific temperament, research, innovation and meaningful service to society. The Foundation seeks to create opportunities for students, young researchers, educators and communities to learn, experiment, collaborate and develop practical solutions for real-world challenges.

The Founders

GARRF has been conceived as a family-led initiative with an emphasis on education, scientific temperament, research and service.

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Founder — Student of Young Economic Times Entrepreneur & Birla Open Minds International School.

His involvement represents the Foundation's commitment to encouraging curiosity, creativity, entrepreneurship and scientific thinking among young students.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Founder & Chief Scientific Officer — Senior Scientist and Singapore returnee, with a strong scientific temperament and extensive academic and research experience.

He is the recipient of the Yashokirti Award 2026 associated with the Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru Alumni Association.

His vision is to promote research, science, innovation and scientific temperament among youth and young students, while developing meaningful opportunities for interdisciplinary learning and collaboration.

Both founders have returned from Singapore, bringing together international academic exposure, research experience and a strong commitment to contributing to education and scientific development in Bharat.

International & National Advisory Network

GARRF is supported by advisors and collaborators with experience spanning Singapore, USA, India and international academic and research institutions.

The GARRF Foundation advisors include professionals with connections to Singapore and USA and individuals affiliated with organisations and institutions including ISRO, IISc and IITs in India. Several advisors possess higher academic degrees from reputed foreign universities.

Collectively, they bring decades of academic, scientific, research and industry experience from India and overseas. Their experience strengthens GARRF's objective of creating meaningful opportunities for students, researchers and young innovators.
