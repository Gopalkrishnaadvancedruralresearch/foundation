Who We Are

A Father–Son Foundation Built on Global Experience, Academic Excellence & Service

Founded by a Father–Son Duo

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was started by the father-and-son duo Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna , with the support, blessings and encouragement of their family.

The Foundation brings together two generations with a shared vision of education, scientific research, technology, entrepreneurship, innovation and service to society.

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna is one of India's youngest CEOs and a Young Economic Times Entrepreneur student . He is also a student of Birla Open Minds International School . His participation reflects GARRF's commitment to encouraging young entrepreneurs, innovators and future leaders.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav — Singapore Returnee & Senior Scientist

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a Singapore returnee who spent more than 20 years overseas , building extensive academic, scientific, research and professional experience. His international journey spans Singapore, the United States and India , with experience in scientific research, engineering, technology, project management, student mentoring, industry collaboration and innovation.

He has guided and mentored hundreds of undergraduate and graduate students , particularly during his years in Singapore, and has developed extensive experience in guiding students towards scientific research, engineering projects, innovation and practical problem solving.

Student Services

Training, Internships, Projects & Mentoring

GARRF provides training and project-oriented services for undergraduate and graduate students. Our approach is based on practical learning, scientific thinking and carefully curated projects. Students from engineering and management programmes may participate subject to the applicable programme, institutional agreement and project requirements.

Undergraduate & Graduate Training

Structured training for genuine students who are willing to work seriously on research, technology and project-oriented learning.

AI Internships

Specialised AI training curated around the student's project, including project-oriented learning and practical implementation.

Final Year Projects

Curated engineering and technology projects designed to encourage serious research, implementation, documentation and presentation.

PhD Research Guidance

Guidance and technical discussion for PhD scholars in coordination with their respective supervisors and institutions.

Student Referral Letters

Eligible students may receive referral or recommendation letters based on their genuine participation, project work, performance and professional relationship with GARRF.

Student Mentoring

Guidance based on extensive experience mentoring undergraduate and graduate students, particularly through international academic and professional exposure in Singapore.

A Serious Project-Oriented Learning Environment

GARRF seeks to work with genuine and motivated students who want to develop projects to a strong academic and practical standard. Students can use the opportunity to learn from experienced researchers and professionals and develop work that demonstrates genuine technical effort.
