International Advisory Network

GARRF is privileged to receive the guidance, encouragement and blessings of distinguished Honorary Advisors whose academic, scientific and professional journeys span India and leading international institutions. Honorary Advisor

Prof. N. Sundararajan

Space Scientist • Academic • Engineering & Research Mentor

Prof. N. Sundararajan brings an exceptional career spanning India's space programme, international research and higher education. His professional journey connects ISRO's Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore and research environments in the United States.

Distinguished Professional Contributions

  • Former SLV-3 Mission Analysis Head, Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC), ISRO.
  • Former Group Director, Launch Vehicle Design Group, VSSC, ISRO.
  • More than two decades of pioneering contribution to India's space programme.
  • Professor (Retd.), Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering.
  • Research Associate at NASA Ames Research Center and NASA Langley Research Center, USA.
  • Expertise associated with computational intelligence, neural networks, machine learning and their engineering applications.

Education

Ph.D. — University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, USA
M.Tech. — IIT Madras, India

Honorary Advisor

Prof. Soh Chee Kiong

Structural Engineering • Structural Health Monitoring • Academic Leadership

Prof. Soh Chee Kiong is an internationally experienced civil and structural engineer, researcher and academic whose career spans industry, NTU Singapore, Southeast University, China and the National University of Singapore.

Distinguished Academic & Professional Journey

  • Adjunct Professor, Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering, National University of Singapore (NUS).
  • Former senior academic leader at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.
  • Former Vice Dean and academic leadership roles in the School of Civil & Structural Engineering at NTU.
  • Former Head of Structures & Construction and research leadership roles within NTU.
  • Distinguished Professor, Southeast University, China.
  • Fellow of the Singapore Academy of Engineering.
  • International engineering experience including offshore structures, marine engineering and structural systems.
  • More than 200 SCI-indexed research papers and internationally recognised contributions to structural engineering and smart structural health monitoring.

Education

B.Eng. — Concordia University, Canada
S.M. — Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), USA
Ph.D. — University of Wales, College of Cardiff, UK
