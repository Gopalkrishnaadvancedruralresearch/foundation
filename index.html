PZT · EMI · SHM

ARJUN — Signal Analyzer

AI assistant dedicated to Electro-Mechanical Impedance (EMI) and Piezoelectric (PZT) structural health monitoring.

Helps students, researchers, and engineers analyse EMI/PZT response signals and focus on actionable research insights.

Launch ARJUN ↗
🔍
DEFECTIVE DIGITAL TWIN

VIRUDH — Visual SHM

Defective Digital Twin (DDT) engine initialized for automated visual structural health inspection.

Upload Before (Healthy) and After (Damaged) surface photos to compute alignment scoring, match percentages, and automated surface crack detection.
