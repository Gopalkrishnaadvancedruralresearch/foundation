From International Research to Rural Impact

The Foundation's vision is shaped by academic, scientific and professional experiences gained across India and overseas. The association with institutions such as Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, together with experience connected with the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, provides an international and interdisciplinary perspective to GARRF.

GARRF seeks to bring this experience closer to young students and researchers in Bharat by encouraging scientific temperament, research, innovation, mentoring and practical problem-solving.
