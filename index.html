Advancing Knowledge. Empowering Communities.

GARRF is an education and research-oriented foundation established to encourage scientific temperament, research, innovation and meaningful service to society. We create opportunities for students, young researchers, educators and institutions to learn, experiment, collaborate and undertake meaningful projects.

Our vision is simple: connect high-quality education, research and practical innovation with the needs of students and rural India.

Who We Are

A Father–Son Vision for Research, Education & Rural Empowerment

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was started by a father–son duo, Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna , with the support and blessings of their family.

The Foundation brings together international academic, scientific, professional and entrepreneurial experience with a strong commitment to students, research, innovation and the development of rural India.

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Shantanu is a young entrepreneur and student, associated with Young Economic Times Entrepreneur and Birla Open Minds International School.

His involvement represents the Foundation's commitment to introducing young minds to entrepreneurship, innovation, scientific thinking and responsible community service.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Dr. Annamdas is a Singapore returnee who spent more than two decades in international academic, scientific and professional environments.

His education and professional journey includes IISc Bengaluru, NTU Singapore, the USA and Singapore, together with extensive experience in research, engineering, project management, student mentoring and technology development.

He is a recipient of the Yashokirti Award 2026 associated with the Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru Alumni Association.

Academic Excellence & International Experience

GARRF draws upon an educational and professional foundation spanning India • Singapore • USA. Dr. Annamdas holds a PhD degree from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore, Master's degree from Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bengaluru, a Project Management MicroMasters from Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), USA, a Postdoctoral experience at the University of Pittsburgh, USA, and professional project-management certification associated with the Project Management Institute (PMI), USA.

Honorary Advisors

Honorary Advisors & International Advisory Network

GARRF is privileged to receive the guidance, encouragement and blessings of distinguished Honorary Advisors with substantial academic, scientific, technological and professional experience. Their qualifications and decades of experience provide an important source of wisdom as the Foundation develops programmes for students, researchers and rural communities.

The advisory network extends across Singapore, the United States and Europe, with academic and professional associations connected with institutions including NTU Singapore, NASA, ISRO, IITs and IISc Bengaluru, together with other leading international institutions.

An Internationally Connected Foundation
GARRF aspires to build one of India's strongest internationally connected advisory foundations for rural development, scientific education and student empowerment . The Foundation's advisory and professional network brings together experience from India, Singapore, USA and Europe , helping undergraduate and graduate students work towards global standards while remaining connected to the needs of rural India.
Student Services

Training, Internships, Projects & Student Mentoring

GARRF provides specialised training and project-oriented opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students in engineering, technology and management disciplines. Our approach is built on practical learning, curated projects, research exposure and mentoring.

★

AI & Specialised Training

Specialised AI training curated around the student's project, including practical applications and project implementation.

✓

Internships

Internship opportunities with structured, project-oriented training for genuine students who are willing to undertake serious technical work.

+

Final-Year Projects

Curated final-year projects designed around meaningful engineering, AI, research and interdisciplinary challenges.

R

Research Mentoring

Guidance for students interested in scientific research, publications, experimentation and developing projects to a high academic standard.

P

PhD Guidance

Research discussions and technical guidance can be provided to PhD scholars in coordination with their respective supervisors.

G

University Collaboration

GARRF can work with universities and institutes on joint projects, research activities, mentoring and industry-linked opportunities.

Student Mentoring Experience: Dr. Annamdas has extensive experience guiding hundreds of undergraduate and graduate students in Singapore and has pioneered project-oriented training towards scientific research. His international academic and professional experience across India, Singapore and the USA is brought into GARRF's student mentoring activities.
Areas of Technical Experience: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Robotics, Structural Health Monitoring, Smart Materials, Piezoelectric Technologies, Android Applications, Arduino and embedded systems, intelligent infrastructure, engineering research, project management and interdisciplinary technology development.
University–Industry Partnership: GARRF is also developing opportunities for joint projects with universities and institutes. Faculty members may participate as collaborators or co-investigators in suitable research initiatives, including projects seeking support from Indian and State Government research programmes, subject to the respective programme rules and institutional agreements.
Student Recommendations & Referrals: Based on genuine academic/project engagement and individual assessment, GARRF may provide appropriate student referral or recommendation letters for higher education, international admissions or industry opportunities. Such recommendations are based on the student's actual work, performance and demonstrated capabilities.
Our Project Philosophy: We aim to help motivated students undertake projects that are academically serious, technically meaningful and comparable in ambition with high-quality projects undertaken at leading institutions. GARRF welcomes students who genuinely want to learn, research and contribute — rather than simply obtain a certificate.
