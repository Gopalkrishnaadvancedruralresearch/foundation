Who We Are

A Father–Son Vision for Education, Research & Rural Empowerment

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was started by a father-and-son duo, Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna, with the support and blessings of their family. The Foundation brings together international scientific experience, academic mentorship, young entrepreneurship and a strong commitment to empowering students and communities in rural India.

The Founders

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a Singapore returnee who spent more than two decades building academic, scientific and professional experience across Singapore, USA and India. His academic journey includes Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore and M.E. from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru . He has also undertaken post-doctoral research at the University of Pittsburgh, USA .

His professional development includes Project Management Professional (PMP®) certification from the Project Management Institute (PMI), USA, together with a Project Management MicroMasters from Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), USA . His career has involved scientific research, structural health monitoring, smart materials, piezoelectric systems, robotics, Arduino, Android applications, artificial intelligence, machine learning and interdisciplinary engineering.

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna represents the Foundation's young-generation entrepreneurship and leadership dimension. He is associated with Young Economic Times Entrepreneur activities and is a student of Birla Open Minds International School . Shantanu also serves as a young entrepreneur and CEO, bringing the perspective of the next generation into the Foundation's mission.

Together, the founders seek to connect global academic experience with Indian educational needs and create meaningful opportunities for students, educators, researchers and rural communities.

Student Services

Training, Internships, Projects & Mentoring

GARRF provides structured educational and research support for undergraduate and graduate students, engineering students, management students, researchers and selected Ph.D. scholars. We focus on genuine students who are willing to work seriously and develop projects to a level comparable with strong students from leading institutions.

Undergraduate & Graduate Training

Training programmes are designed for undergraduate and graduate students in engineering, technology, management and related disciplines. Training is project-oriented and intended to develop scientific thinking, practical skills and confidence.

AI Internship & Specialised Training

We provide specialised AI-oriented internships and training curated around individual projects. The training may include artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science and intelligent applications.

Final-Year Projects

Students can undertake curated final-year projects designed around real engineering, technology, AI and interdisciplinary challenges. Projects are undertaken subject to agreement with the student's university or institute.

Research Mentoring

Students interested in scientific research receive guidance in research methodology, project formulation, technical writing, literature review and experimental thinking.

Ph.D. Research Guidance

Selected Ph.D. students may receive research guidance and technical mentoring in coordination with their respective supervisors and institutions.

Academic & Professional Referrals

Where appropriate and based on genuine academic or project performance, students may receive referral or recommendation letters for further academic opportunities, foreign admissions and industry applications.

Student Mentoring Experience

Dr. Annamdas brings extensive experience in guiding students, particularly undergraduate and graduate students, during his years of academic and professional work in Singapore and India. His mentoring approach is strongly project-oriented: students are encouraged to understand the problem, study the scientific background, develop a solution, document their work and communicate their results professionally.

  • Experience guiding hundreds of students and young researchers.
  • Strong experience in Structural Health Monitoring and smart engineering systems.
  • Experience with smart materials and piezoelectric technologies.
  • Experience in robotics, Arduino and engineering applications.
  • Experience with Android applications and technology development.
  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and interdisciplinary research experience.
  • International academic and professional exposure across Singapore, USA and India.
  • Project-management expertise supported by PMP® certification and international project experience.

Students who demonstrate genuine commitment, technical ability and good conduct may also be considered for appropriate academic or professional referrals. Recommendation letters are issued based on the student's actual work, performance and suitability; GARRF does not issue automatic endorsements.

How Students Can Join

GARRF normally works with universities and institutions through academic discussions and project arrangements. A significant proportion of opportunities are introduced through visits, discussions and institutional engagement. A limited number of opportunities may also be available for students who approach GARRF directly.

Students may undertake projects for an agreed fee, where applicable, based on the nature of the project and the agreement with their respective university or institute. GARRF currently places strong emphasis on online mentoring and project-based training , while continuing to explore institutional and on-site opportunities.

For student opportunities, vacancies and project enquiries: founder@garrf.in   |   help_desk@garrf.in

Industry–University & Joint Research Projects

GARRF is not limited to student internships. We also seek to create meaningful partnerships between universities, faculty members, researchers, industry and government-oriented research opportunities.

  • Faculty members may explore joint projects and collaborative research with GARRF.
  • Faculty can potentially participate as co-investigators or collaborators in suitable research proposals, subject to institutional rules and funding-agency requirements.
  • GARRF seeks industry partnerships that can connect universities with practical engineering and technology challenges.
  • Joint proposals may be explored for suitable opportunities from Indian central and state government research and innovation programmes.
  • Students can benefit from projects that combine academic learning with real-world industry requirements.

Our objective is to create an ecosystem in which faculty, students, researchers and industry can work together on meaningful projects while contributing to India's scientific and rural development goals.

Our Larger Mission

Global Standards. Indian Roots. Rural Impact.

GARRF believes that high-quality education, scientific thinking and project-based learning should not be restricted to students in a few privileged institutions. We aspire to help students across India develop capabilities that allow them to participate confidently in global research and technology.

At the same time, the knowledge and innovation generated through these activities should ultimately contribute to rural India, sustainable development and community empowerment .
