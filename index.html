ABOUT GARRF

Knowledge with Purpose.
Research with Impact.

The GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is an education and research-oriented foundation established with the belief that education, science and innovation should ultimately contribute to society.

GARRF seeks to nurture scientific temperament among school and university students, young researchers and aspiring innovators. Through mentoring, research activities, projects, collaborations and knowledge-sharing initiatives, the Foundation aims to connect learning with practical challenges.

A special emphasis is placed on bringing experienced scientists, researchers, academics and professionals together with young learners so that knowledge can move between generations and across geographical boundaries.

THE FOUNDATION'S BEGINNING

Dr. Annamdas and His Family

GARRF emerged from a family vision combining international research experience, education, scientific curiosity and a commitment to creating opportunities for young minds.

FOUNDER

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Student of Young Economic Times Entrepreneur & Birla Open Minds International School

Shantanu represents the young generation of the Foundation and its belief that curiosity, creativity and scientific thinking can be encouraged from an early stage.

His involvement reflects the Foundation's commitment to creating an environment where young learners can observe, question, explore, experiment and gradually develop an interest in science, technology, innovation and responsible leadership.

FOUNDER & CHIEF SCIENTIFIC OFFICER

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Er. Avinash Shirode "Yashokirti" Awardee 2026
Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru Alumni Association

Dr. Annamdas is a Senior Scientist and Singapore Returnee whose professional journey includes academic, scientific and research associations with Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore , together with academic and professional experience in the USA.

He brings a strong scientific temperament and international research experience to GARRF. His motivation in establishing the Foundation is to promote research, science and scientific thinking among youth and young students .

A SHARED JOURNEY

Both Returned From Singapore

The Foundation's story is also shaped by a return from Singapore to India. The experience of living and learning in an international research environment helped strengthen the belief that global knowledge can be connected with India's own educational, scientific and community needs.

GARRF therefore seeks to create a bridge between international academic and professional experience and the aspirations of students, young researchers and communities in Bharat.

A Family Vision for Science and Education

GARRF has been founded by Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna and Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav , bringing together the enthusiasm of young leadership and the experience of a senior scientist and researcher.

Dr. Annamdas is a Senior Scientist and Singapore Returnee . His scientific journey across countries and institutions strengthened his belief that young students should be exposed to science not merely as a subject to study, but as a way of thinking — a process of questioning, observing, experimenting, analysing and creating.

This scientific temperament became one of the important motivations behind GARRF: to encourage research, science and scientific thinking among youth and young students , while creating opportunities for learning, experimentation, mentoring and innovation.

The Foundation seeks to bring together experienced scientists, researchers, educators and young learners, creating a platform where knowledge can move between generations and across geographical boundaries.

The GARRF advisory network includes professionals with experience spanning Singapore, USA and India , including individuals associated with ISRO, IISc and IITs , together with researchers and professionals holding higher degrees from universities and institutions outside India.

Their decades of academic, scientific and industry experience provide an important foundation for mentoring young students, researchers and innovators.

OUR VISION

Science for Young Minds

GARRF believes that scientific curiosity should begin early. Young students should be encouraged not merely to learn existing knowledge, but also to question, investigate, experiment and create.

The Foundation seeks to provide an environment in which young people can discover their potential as scientists, engineers, researchers and innovators. Through mentorship, projects, research exposure and collaboration with experienced professionals, GARRF aims to make scientific learning more accessible and meaningful.

At the same time, GARRF seeks to connect international academic and professional experience with the needs of communities in Bharat. The long-term objective is to encourage affordable, practical and sustainable technologies that can create meaningful impact.
