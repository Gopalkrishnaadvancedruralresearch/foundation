Advancing Knowledge.

GARRF is an education and research-oriented foundation established to encourage scientific temperament, research, innovation and meaningful service to society. The Foundation seeks to create opportunities for students, young researchers, educators and communities to learn, experiment, collaborate and develop practical solutions for real-world challenges.

A Foundation Built on International Experience

Both founders have a connection with Singapore and international academic and professional environments. This international exposure forms an important part of the Foundation's outlook towards education, research, innovation and collaboration.

The GARRF advisory ecosystem brings together professionals and academics associated with Singapore and the USA, including individuals affiliated with ISRO and alumni of leading Indian institutions such as IISc and IITs, together with higher academic qualifications from universities abroad.

Collectively, the advisors bring decades of academic, scientific, engineering and industry experience from India and overseas. GARRF seeks to use this experience to guide students, young researchers and emerging innovators.
