Who We Are

A Family Foundation with International Academic & Scientific Experience

Father–Son Founders

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna established GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation with the support and blessings of their family.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a Singapore returnee who spent more than two decades in international scientific, academic and professional environments. His experience includes research, engineering, artificial intelligence, structural health monitoring, smart materials, robotics, project management and student mentoring.

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna represents the Foundation's emphasis on the next generation. He is associated with the Young Economic Times Entrepreneur programme and is a student of Birla Open Minds International School. His involvement reflects GARRF's commitment to entrepreneurship, innovation, education and youth leadership.

Together, the founders seek to connect global knowledge, Indian academic excellence, student development and rural empowerment.

Student Services

Training, Internships, Projects & Research Mentoring

GARRF provides specialised training and project opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students across engineering and management disciplines. The Foundation builds upon extensive experience in guiding hundreds of students, particularly undergraduate and graduate students, during international academic and professional engagement in Singapore.

Our objective is simple: help genuinely motivated students undertake meaningful projects and develop work that approaches global academic and professional standards.

Internships & Specialised Training

GARRF offers internships and specialised training curated according to the student's project and academic requirements.

AI-focused training can be designed around specific engineering projects, including practical applications and research-oriented development.

Opportunities are offered subject to project requirements, availability and appropriate agreements with the respective university or institute.

Final-Year & Academic Projects

Students may undertake final-year projects, research projects and specialised technical projects under guidance from experienced researchers and professionals.

Projects may involve AI, robotics, SHM, smart materials, piezoelectric systems, Arduino, embedded systems, applications and other interdisciplinary technologies.

Project opportunities are subject to academic requirements, project suitability and mutually agreed terms with universities or institutes.

Research & PhD Guidance

GARRF can also support PhD scholars and research students in consultation with their academic supervisors.

The emphasis is on genuine research, scientific methodology, interdisciplinary thinking and meaningful outcomes.

Academic & Professional Referrals

Where appropriate and based on genuine academic or project engagement, students may receive referral or recommendation letters for further academic or professional opportunities.

Such recommendations are based on the student's actual performance, contribution, conduct and demonstrated capabilities.

Students are encouraged to build a genuine professional profile and seek appropriate endorsements through professional networks such as LinkedIn.

University & Faculty Collaboration

GARRF is open to joint projects with universities and institutes. Faculty members may collaborate with the Foundation on suitable research and project initiatives.

Where appropriate, faculty collaborators may work with GARRF as co-investigators or research partners on proposals and grant opportunities with Indian central and state government agencies, subject to the applicable rules and eligibility requirements.

GARRF also seeks to develop industry–university partnerships that can provide students with exposure to practical engineering, technology and management challenges.

We normally engage with universities and institutes directly to discuss project opportunities. A limited number of opportunities may also be available to students who approach GARRF directly.
