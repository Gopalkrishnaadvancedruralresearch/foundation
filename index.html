❓ How to Use the Assistant (Step-by-Step Guide for Students)

  • 1
    Launch the Assistant

    Click the "Launch AI Assistant" button above to open the dedicated GARRF Gem interface in a new window.

  • 2
    Upload Your Signal Files

    Attach or upload your signal files—accepts screenshots, plot images, PDF reports, Excel files, or CSV exports.

  • 3
    Handling Differing Frequency Ranges (Important Note)

    If the frequency ranges of your uploaded signals do not match (e.g., 10–100 Hz vs. 50–90 Hz), simply state in your prompt which signal is the Healthy Baseline (Stage 0) and which are the Later-Staged / Damaged Signals (or vice versa).

    💡 Simple Prompt Example: "Image 1 is my healthy baseline signal (10–100 Hz). Image 2 is my later-stage damaged signal (50–90 Hz). Please align the frequency domain and generate the health dashboard."
  • 4
    Review Your Diagnostic Dashboard

    The AI will automatically crop the matching frequency intersection window, align the data points, and display your full RMSD/MAPD metrics and structural damage rating.
