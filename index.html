ABOUT GARRF

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is committed to advancing education, scientific research, innovation and rural development.

The Foundation seeks to create opportunities for students, researchers and communities by connecting knowledge with practical applications and meaningful social impact.

GARRF promotes a culture of learning, research, integrity, collaboration and service, with a particular focus on empowering rural communities and nurturing the next generation of scientists, researchers and innovators.
