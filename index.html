Prof. N. Sundararajan

Honorary Advisor • GARRF

Distinguished Space Scientist • Academician • Mentor

Prof. N. Sundararajan is a distinguished aerospace scientist and academic whose career bridges India's space programme, NASA research and advanced computational intelligence.

Education

  • B.E. Electrical Engineering, First Class Honours — University of Madras, 1966
  • M.Tech — IIT Madras, 1968
  • Ph.D. Electrical Engineering — University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 1971

Space Science & ISRO Leadership

  • Served in the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) from 1972–1991.
  • Progressed from Control System Designer to Director, Launch Vehicle Design Group.
  • Contributed to the design and development of India's satellite launch vehicles including SLV-3, ASLV, PSLV and GSLV.
  • Served as Project Engineer (Mission) in the first SLV-3 project team, working directly under Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam.

NASA & International Research

  • NRC Research Associate, NASA Ames Research Center, 1974.
  • Senior Research Associate, NASA Langley Research Center, 1981–1986.
  • Served as Professor in the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering, NTU Singapore.
  • Also served as an endowed Prof. I. G. Sarma Memorial ARDB Professor at IISc Bangalore.

Research & Recognition

  • Research interests include aerospace control, neural networks, machine learning and computational intelligence.
  • Author of multiple books and 250+ research publications across his career.
  • Life Fellow of IEEE.
  • Associate Fellow of AIAA.
  • Fellow of the Institution of Engineers, Singapore.

Prof. Soh Chee Kiong

Honorary Advisor • GARRF

Distinguished Engineer • Academic Leader • Mentor

Prof. Soh Chee Kiong is a distinguished civil and structural engineering academic whose career spans advanced offshore engineering, structural health monitoring, smart materials, sustainable urban systems and engineering education.

Education

  • B.Eng. — Concordia University, Canada
  • S.M. — Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), USA
  • Ph.D. — University of Wales, College of Cardiff, UK

Academic Leadership — NTU Singapore

  • Long-serving Professor at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.
  • Former Vice Dean of the School of Civil & Structural Engineering.
  • Former Head of the Division of Structures & Construction and Division of Structures & Mechanics.
  • Former Assistant Director of Research, NTU Office of Research.
  • Former Chair, School of Civil & Environmental Engineering.

International Engineering Experience

  • Worked as an offshore engineer with McDermott South East Asia.
  • Worked as a marine surveyor with Noble Denton & Associates Singapore.
  • Designed, fabricated, installed and commissioned more than a dozen offshore platforms in Southeast Asian waters.
  • Contributed to the laying of the then world's largest submarine pipeline in the E11 gas field off Bintulu for Sarawak Shell.

Research & Global Recognition

  • Research includes smart materials, structural health monitoring, damage prognosis, energy harvesting and sustainable urban systems.
  • Author/co-author of 200+ SCI papers.
  • Recipient of major structural engineering and research paper awards.
  • Fellow of the Singapore Academy of Engineering since 2012.
  • Registered Professional Engineer of Singapore.
  • Distinguished Professor, Southeast University, China.
  • Adjunct Professor, National University of Singapore.
