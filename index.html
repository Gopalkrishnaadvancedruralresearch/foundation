A Father–Son Research Initiative

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation

GARRF

A knowledge-driven foundation bringing together generations, science, technology, education, and innovation to create meaningful solutions for rural communities and a sustainable future. Through our initiatives, students in India are given opportunities to work on projects based on projects executed at leading global universities such as Stanford, MIT, Cambridge, Harvard, NTU Singapore, and NUS Singapore. They receive the guidance, mentorship, and training required to execute these projects to international academic, research, and industry standards. Our students also work on real-world, industry-relevant projects aligned with current and emerging job requirements, while developing globally relevant research skills. Our aim is to equip students with the practical experience, research capabilities, technological expertise, and problem-solving skills needed to become industry-ready, research-ready, and globally competitive.

ARJUN — AI Assistant for Piezoelectric SHM

We are proud to introduce GARRF AI EMI Signal Analyzer — ARJUN, an AI assistant dedicated specifically to Electro-Mechanical Impedance (EMI) and Piezoelectric (PZT) Structural Health Monitoring.

Research and experimentation often involve messy data, overlapping plots, and mismatched frequency windows. ARJUN is designed to reduce these manual challenges so students, researchers, and engineers can focus on meaningful insights and analysis.

VIRUDH — AI Defective Digital Twin (Visual Inspection)

By Dr. Venu Gopal Madhav Annamdas

Defective Digital Twin (DDT) initialized for visual inspection. Asks researchers and students to upload Before (Healthy) and After (Damaged) surface photos.

Provides alignment scoring (%), structural match scores (%), and delta extraction to detect surface cracks or spalling.
