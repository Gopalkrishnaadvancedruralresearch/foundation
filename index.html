GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is an education and research-oriented foundation established to nurture scientific temperament, encourage innovation and create meaningful opportunities for students, researchers, educators and communities.

Our vision is simple: to connect education, research, industry and society while helping young people undertake meaningful projects and develop solutions that can contribute to a stronger and more self-reliant rural India.

Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Family, Global Experience & Knowledge

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was started by a father-and-son duo, Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna , with the support and blessings of their family. The Foundation brings together academic experience, international professional exposure, student mentoring, scientific research and a strong commitment to rural empowerment.

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna is a young entrepreneur and student associated with Young Economic Times Entrepreneur and Birla Open Minds International School.

His participation in GARRF represents the Foundation's commitment to involving young people in entrepreneurship, innovation, education and responsible service to society. He brings a youthful perspective to the Foundation's vision of encouraging the next generation to think beyond conventional boundaries.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a Singapore returnee who spent more than two decades overseas as a scientist, researcher, academic and engineering professional.

His academic and professional journey spans Singapore, the United States and India , with education and professional experience associated with NTU Singapore, IISc Bengaluru, University of Pittsburgh, Rochester Institute of Technology and Project Management Institute, USA.

He is a recipient of the Yashokirti Award 2026 associated with the Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru Alumni Association.

Honorary Advisors & International Advisory Network

GARRF is privileged to receive the guidance, encouragement and blessings of distinguished Honorary Advisors with substantial academic, scientific, technological and professional experience. Their qualifications and decades of experience provide an important source of wisdom as the Foundation develops its programmes for students and rural communities.

The advisory network includes distinguished professionals and academics associated with institutions and professional communities in Singapore, the United States and Europe, including experience connected with NTU Singapore, NASA, ISRO, IITs, IISc Bengaluru and other leading academic and scientific institutions.

The Foundation also benefits from the association and guidance of distinguished academic leaders, including Prof. Soh Chee Kiong , together with other Honorary Advisors whose educational and professional backgrounds span advanced research, engineering, space science, artificial intelligence and academia.

Our aspiration is to build one of India's strongest internationally connected advisory foundations for rural development, scientific education and student empowerment — helping undergraduate and graduate students work towards global standards while remaining connected to the needs of rural India.

A Different Approach to Student Projects

GARRF believes that a project should be more than a document submitted for examination. Students are encouraged to understand the problem, study the scientific principles, develop the technology, implement the solution and explain their work confidently.

The Foundation has experience guiding students towards scientific research and practical engineering projects. Our objective is to help committed students raise the quality of their work towards global academic and professional standards.

Project opportunities may be offered through institutional arrangements with universities and colleges. A limited number of opportunities may also be made available directly to students who approach GARRF and satisfy the required criteria.

Interested students and institutions: contact us for upcoming project, internship and training opportunities.

Joint Projects with Universities & Institutes

GARRF is also developing partnerships with educational institutions and industry to create meaningful opportunities for students and faculty. Universities and institutes may collaborate with GARRF on research projects, student projects, technology development, innovation and applied research.

Faculty members may participate as co-investigators or research collaborators on suitable joint proposals, subject to the requirements of the respective institutions and funding agencies.

Industry–University Partnership: GARRF seeks to connect academic institutions with practical industry-oriented research and technology development. We aim to help universities create projects that have genuine technical value while also exploring opportunities for research proposals to appropriate Indian and state government programmes.
