ABOUT GARRF

Knowledge with Purpose.
Research with Impact.

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is committed to creating an environment where education, scientific research, innovation and community development come together.

The Foundation seeks to encourage students, researchers, educators and communities to explore ideas, develop practical solutions and contribute meaningfully to society.
