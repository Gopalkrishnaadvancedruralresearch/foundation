🚀 WORLD'S FIRST AI ASSISTANT · PZT · EMI · STRUCTURAL HEALTH MONITORING

ARJUN — GARRF AI Piezoelectric Transducer based & Electromechanical Impedance Signal Analyzer

FAST RESULTS · FREE · OPEN-SOURCE EDUCATIONAL TOOL

🚀 News for the Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) and research community!

We are proud to introduce ARJUN, the GARRF AI EMI & PZT Signal Analyzer — the world's first AI assistant dedicated specifically to Electro-Mechanical Impedance (EMI) and Piezoelectric (PZT) Structural Health Monitoring.

Research and experimentation often come with messy data, overlapping plots and mismatched frequency windows. ARJUN is designed to reduce those manual tasks so students, researchers and engineers can focus on the insights and analysis.

Developed under GARRF, this open-source educational tool is intended to make advanced SHM diagnostics more accessible, especially for students and researchers.

As a free, open-source educational tool, ARJUN is designed to help make advanced EMI/PZT diagnostics accessible to students, researchers and engineers. Follow Gopalkrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) on LinkedIn and be part of our journey to bring cutting-edge technologies closer to rural students.

DEFECTIVE DIGITAL TWIN · VISUAL SHM

VIRUDH — AI Defective Digital Twin (Visual Inspection)

By Dr. Venu Gopal Madhav Annamdas

Defective Digital Twin (DDT) initialized for visual inspection. Asks researchers and students to upload Before (Healthy) and After (Damaged) surface photos.

Provides alignment scoring (%), structural match scores (%), and delta extraction to detect surface cracks or spalling.
