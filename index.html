VIRUDH — AI Defective Digital Twin (Visual Inspection)

By Dr. Venu Gopal Madhav Annamdas

Defective Digital Twin (DDT) initialized for visual inspection. Asks researchers and students to upload Before (Healthy) and After (Damaged) surface photos.

Provides alignment scoring (%), structural match scores (%), and delta extraction to detect surface cracks or spalling.
