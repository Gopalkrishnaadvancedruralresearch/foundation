ABOUT GARRF

Knowledge with Purpose.
Research with Impact.

The GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was established with the belief that education, scientific research and innovation should ultimately contribute to society. The Foundation brings together ideas from education, science, engineering, technology and community development to encourage young minds to become researchers, innovators and responsible contributors to society.

GARRF particularly seeks to nurture scientific temperament among school and university students, young researchers and aspiring innovators. Through mentoring, research activities, projects, collaborations and knowledge-sharing initiatives, the Foundation aims to connect learning with practical challenges.

The Foundation also seeks to build bridges between India's emerging talent and experienced researchers and professionals with international academic and industry experience.

A Family Vision for Science and Education

GARRF has been founded by Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna and Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav, bringing together the enthusiasm of young leadership and the experience of a senior scientist and researcher.

Dr. Annamdas is a Senior Scientist and Singapore Returnee whose professional journey has included academic, scientific and research associations with Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, as well as research and professional experience in the USA.

His scientific journey across countries and institutions strengthened his belief that young students should be exposed to science not merely as a subject to study, but as a way of thinking — a process of questioning, observing, experimenting, analysing and creating.

This scientific temperament became one of the important motivations behind GARRF: to encourage research, science and scientific thinking among youth and young students, while creating opportunities for learning, experimentation, mentoring and innovation.

The Foundation seeks to bring together experienced scientists, researchers, educators and young learners, creating a platform where knowledge can move between generations and across geographical boundaries.

Dr. Annamdas is also an Er. Avinash Shirode "Yashokirti" Awardee 2026 from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, recognising his scientific and professional contributions.

NEXT GENERATION

Encouraging Young Minds

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna represents the Foundation's emphasis on encouraging young minds to develop curiosity, creativity, leadership and an interest in science and technology.

GARRF believes that the next generation should not merely inherit existing knowledge. Young learners should be encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, experiment, learn from failure and develop solutions of their own.

Through the Foundation's education, research and mentoring initiatives, young students can gradually become familiar with scientific thinking, interdisciplinary learning and responsible innovation.

OUR VISION

Science for Young Minds

GARRF believes that scientific curiosity should begin early. Young students should be encouraged not merely to learn existing knowledge, but also to question, investigate, experiment and create.

The Foundation seeks to provide an environment in which young people can discover their potential as scientists, engineers, researchers and innovators. Through mentorship, projects, research exposure and collaboration with experienced professionals, GARRF aims to make scientific learning more accessible and meaningful.

At the same time, GARRF seeks to connect international academic and professional experience with the needs of communities in Bharat. The long-term objective is to encourage affordable, practical and sustainable technologies that can create meaningful impact.
