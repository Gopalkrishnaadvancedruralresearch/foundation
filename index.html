Who We Are

A Father–Son Vision for Research, Education & Rural Empowerment

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was started by a father–son duo, Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav and Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna , with the support and blessings of their family.

The Foundation brings together international academic, scientific, professional and entrepreneurial experience with a strong commitment to students, research, innovation and the development of rural India.

Annamdas Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Shantanu is a young entrepreneur and student, associated with Young Economic Times Entrepreneur and Birla Open Minds International School.

His involvement represents the Foundation's commitment to introducing young minds to entrepreneurship, innovation, scientific thinking and responsible community service.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Dr. Annamdas is a Singapore returnee who spent more than two decades in international academic, scientific and professional environments.

His education and professional journey includes IISc Bengaluru, NTU Singapore, the USA and Singapore, together with extensive experience in research, engineering, project management, student mentoring and technology development.

He is a recipient of the Yashokirti Award 2026 associated with the Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru Alumni Association.
