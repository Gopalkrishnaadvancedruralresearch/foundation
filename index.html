ROBOTICS AI ASSISTANT

CHITTI — Our Robotics AI Assistant

To support our robotics work, we developed CHITTI, an AI assistant for robotics learning, project development and experimentation.

CHITTI is designed to help students understand robotics, explore ideas, develop projects and experiment with robotics-related technologies.

AI Maa — Your 200-Hour Project Assistant
