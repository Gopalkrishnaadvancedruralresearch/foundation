About the Foundation

Building a Culture of Scientific Temperament

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) was established with the vision of creating an environment where young students, learners and aspiring researchers can explore science, technology, innovation and interdisciplinary research.

The Foundation seeks to bridge the gap between education, research and real-world applications by encouraging students to ask questions, investigate problems, experiment with ideas and develop solutions that can benefit society.

GARRF particularly aims to encourage young minds from rural and emerging communities by exposing them to research, scientific thinking, technology and international academic perspectives.

Our Founders

The People Behind GARRF

FOUNDER

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna is a student of Young Economic Times Entrepreneur and Birla Open Minds International School.

His involvement represents the Foundation's commitment to encouraging young students to develop curiosity, entrepreneurship, creativity and an early interest in innovation and research.

GARRF believes that young learners should not merely receive knowledge, but should also be encouraged to question, explore, create and contribute.

FOUNDER & CHIEF SCIENTIFIC OFFICER

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a senior scientist and Singapore returnee with a strong academic and research background.

He is a Yashokirti Awardee 2026 of the Indian Institute of Science Bangalore Alumni Association.

His scientific temperament and long-standing academic, research and industry experience inspired the creation of GARRF with the objective of promoting research, science and innovation among youth and young students.

International Perspective

Experienced Advisors & Collaborators

The GARRF Foundation is supported by advisors and collaborators with experience from Singapore and the USA, as well as individuals affiliated with or having backgrounds connected to institutions such as ISRO, IISc and IITs in India.

Several members of the wider advisory network possess higher academic qualifications from universities outside India and bring decades of experience in academia, scientific research, engineering and industry.

Their collective experience provides GARRF with an international perspective and helps create opportunities for students and young researchers to learn from established scientists, engineers, researchers and professionals.
