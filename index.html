🚀 WORLD'S FIRST AI ASSISTANT · PZT · EMI · STRUCTURAL HEALTH MONITORING

ARJUN — GARRF AI Piezoelectric Transducer based & Electromechanical Impedance Signal Analyzer

🚀 News for the Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) and research community!

We are proud to introduce ARJUN, the GARRF AI EMI & PZT Signal Analyzer — the world's first AI assistant dedicated specifically to Electro-Mechanical Impedance (EMI) and Piezoelectric (PZT) Structural Health Monitoring.

Research and experimentation often come with messy data, overlapping plots and mismatched frequency windows. ARJUN is designed to reduce those manual tasks so students, researchers and engineers can focus on the insights and analysis.
