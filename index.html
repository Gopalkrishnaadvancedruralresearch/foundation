About the Foundation

Science, Research and Young Minds

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is envisioned as an education and research-oriented foundation dedicated to nurturing scientific temperament among young students and researchers.

The Foundation encourages curiosity, experimentation, interdisciplinary learning, research collaboration and practical innovation. Its objective is to help young learners move beyond conventional education and develop the confidence to explore scientific and technological challenges.

GARRF aims to build meaningful connections between education, research, industry and society while promoting affordable and sustainable solutions for communities, particularly in rural and developing environments.

The Founders

A Family Vision for Research and Education

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna

Shantanu Vasudev Krishna is a student of Young Economic Times Entrepreneur and Birla Open Minds International School.

His involvement in GARRF reflects the Foundation's emphasis on introducing young minds to entrepreneurship, scientific thinking, innovation and responsible community service.

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav is a senior scientist and Singapore returnee with a strong scientific temperament and extensive academic, research and professional experience.

He established GARRF with the vision of promoting research and science among youth and young students, while creating opportunities for international academic collaboration and practical innovation.

He is the recipient of the Yashokirti Award 2026 associated with the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru Alumni Association.

Both founders returned from Singapore. Their international academic and professional exposure contributes to the Foundation's vision of connecting global knowledge with the needs and aspirations of students, researchers and communities in Bharat.

Academic & Professional Network

GARRF has a strong Indian academic foundation, with members and advisors connected with the alumni and academic communities of premier Indian institutions, including the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru.

The Foundation's wider academic and professional network also includes experience associated with institutions in Singapore and the United States, including Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore , and academic exposure connected with leading American universities such as Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and University of Pittsburgh, among others.

This combination of Indian academic excellence and international exposure is intended to help GARRF create meaningful opportunities for students, young researchers and educators while encouraging collaboration, mentorship, scientific thinking and knowledge exchange.

The Foundation brings together academic, scientific and industry experience accumulated over decades, with the aim of making such knowledge accessible to the next generation.
