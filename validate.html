🏆 Documented priority claims

FIRST REPORTED REAL-WORLD APPLICATION

PZT/EMI Structural Health Monitoring of a Civil Engineering Structure

2010 online publication • Singapore MRT excavation-support structure

The paper Practical implementation of piezo-impedance sensors in monitoring of excavation support structures reports application of PZT-based electromechanical impedance (EMI) monitoring to an actual underground excavation-support structure. The article states that real-life implementation for underground support structures had not previously been done.

The author's deposited manuscript describes this as the first application of PZT sensors in civil engineering using EMI.
Wiley / DOI Paper / Figures
EARLY PIONEERING CONTRIBUTION

Low-Cost Piezoelectric Diaphragm for Real-Time Load Monitoring

2017 • Annamdas & C. K. Soh

Dr. Venu Gopal Madhav Annamdas and Prof. C. K. Soh pioneered the use of a low-cost piezoelectric diaphragm as a calibrated impedance-based sensor for structural load/strain monitoring. Their work demonstrated real-time load monitoring and presented the diaphragm as a lower-cost alternative to conventional PZT/MFC sensing approaches.

Recommended priority wording: “Pioneering use of low-cost piezoelectric diaphragms for real-time structural load monitoring.”

The 2017 Smart Materials and Structures paper reports calibrated piezo-diaphragm impedance sensing, real-time load/strain monitoring and wireless sensing. This page does not make the broader unsupported claim that piezoelectric diaphragms were first used for any loading application worldwide.

2017 DOI Publication list
FIRST OF ITS KIND IN THE WORLD

Metamaterial-Based Load Monitoring of Engineering Beams

2015 • Annamdas & C. K. Soh

The 2015 Structural Health Monitoring conference paper demonstrated a passive metamaterial approach for monitoring loading of aluminium beams. It tested fixed-fixed and simply supported beams and included a configuration in which the metamaterial was not in contact with the structure.

The published abstract explicitly describes the adaptation of metamaterials for monitoring load on aluminium beams as “the first of its kind in the world.”
Official paper page DOI
PIONEERING

Contactless Near-Field Metamaterial SHM

2015–2016 • Annamdas & C. K. Soh

The 2015 precursor included a non-contact metamaterial configuration. The 2016 journal work then developed contactless load monitoring in the near field using surface-localized spoof plasmons—a metamaterial approach for the health monitoring of engineering structures.

2016 DOI 2015 precursor
PIONEERING

Piezoelectric + Metamaterial Electromagnetic Sensing

2016–2017

The research combined piezo-impedance sensing with electromagnetic contact sensing using metamaterial surface plasmons for load monitoring. This is a distinctive bridge between PZT/EMI and metamaterial sensing.

2017 journal record Publication list
EARLY PIONEERING WORK

Robotic-Arm-Based Metamaterial SHM

2018

Research with Prof. Soh applied metamaterial surface plasmons and waveguides to robotic-arm-based structural health monitoring. Because this is a narrow historical intersection, this page labels it “early pioneering” rather than making an unsupported absolute first claim.

Springer DOI Publication list
