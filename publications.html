Our Publications

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is committed to producing impactful research that addresses real-world challenges in engineering, agriculture, artificial intelligence, healthcare, management, education, environmental sustainability, and rural development.

Our publications include peer-reviewed journal articles, conference proceedings, technical reports, books, book chapters, patents, white papers, and collaborative research outputs developed with academic institutions, industry experts, and international collaborators.
