⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE: Hardware Execution Steps

  1. Step 1 (Phone 1 - Virat): Initiates excitation sequence by playing an acoustic sweep through the bonded PZT and logging the start timestamp (T_start), saving the initial reference signal.
  2. Step 2 (Phone 2 - Robin): Operates continuously in recording mode to capture audio wave responses from the secondary PZT patch while logging T_start and audio data.
  3. Step 3 (Phone 2 - Robin): Exports recorded waveform data into robin_catch.csv upon sweep completion.
  4. Step 4 (User Transfer & Phone 1 - Virat): User transfers robin_catch.csv from Phone 2 to Phone 1, where Virat imports the dataset.
  5. Step 5 (Phone 1 - Virat): Executes complete SHM processing pipeline:
    • Cross-Correlation (Time-of-Flight analysis)
    • Fast Fourier Transform (Frequency Response Function / Attenuation)
    • Damage Metric evaluation against structural baseline
