DR. VENU AI — TODAY'S LIP-SYNC DEMO

Files:
1. Dr_Venu_AI_LipSync_Today.ipynb
2. avatar_source_30s.mp4

HOW TO USE:
- Open the .ipynb in Google Colab.
- Run the cells from top to bottom.
- When Colab asks, upload avatar_source_30s.mp4.
- The notebook creates a male Indian-English system voice.
- Wav2Lip then synchronizes your mouth to that voice.
- The final file is /content/dr_venu_ai_lipsync.mp4 and is downloaded by the last cell.

IMPORTANT:
This is a personal/family demonstration. The original open-source Wav2Lip release
states that its open-source model is for personal/research/non-commercial use.
For future public/commercial GARRF use, we should switch to a properly licensed
commercial lip-sync solution.
