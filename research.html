GARRF RESEARCH & INNOVATION

Research for Knowledge, Technology & Impact

GARRF promotes interdisciplinary research connecting engineering, artificial intelligence, sensing, robotics, digital technologies and sustainable development. The objective is to transform research ideas into knowledge, prototypes, publications and meaningful solutions for society and rural communities.

RESEARCH • INNOVATION • EDUCATION • RURAL IMPACT
OUR RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

From Scientific Question to Real-World Solution

GARRF's research approach brings together fundamental understanding, engineering practice, computational intelligence, experimentation and technology development.

The research ecosystem encourages students, researchers, academics and collaborators to explore problems that sit at the intersection of science, engineering and society.

Particular emphasis is placed on interdisciplinary work where Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, sensors, Internet of Things, robotics, embedded systems and engineering knowledge can work together to address complex real-world problems.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING & INTELLIGENT MONITORING

Structural Health Monitoring & Smart Infrastructure

From Sensors to Intelligent Structural Decisions

Structural Health Monitoring is an important research direction connecting structural engineering with sensing, data analytics, artificial intelligence and real-time monitoring.

  • Sensor-based structural data acquisition and monitoring.
  • Data-driven identification of structural behaviour and potential anomalies.
  • Integration of SHM with artificial intelligence and predictive analytics.
  • Smart Real-Time Urban Monitoring (SRUM) concepts.
  • Digital technologies for infrastructure condition assessment and maintenance.

ADVANCED RESEARCH CONCEPT

AI, Smart Infrastructure & Intelligent Thermal Systems

Smart AI Thermal Tiles: Futuristic Cooling With Automated Ventilation for Variable Air Volume Using Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

A research direction combining smart infrastructure, artificial intelligence, automated ventilation, sensing and intelligent control. The concept explores how intelligent systems can respond dynamically to changing environmental and operational conditions.

This type of interdisciplinary research brings together AI, reinforcement learning, sensing, building systems, simulation and real-time control.

AI & Reinforcement Learning Intelligent decision-making and adaptive control.
Sensors & Monitoring Continuous environmental and system-level information.
Smart Ventilation Automated response to changing system requirements.
