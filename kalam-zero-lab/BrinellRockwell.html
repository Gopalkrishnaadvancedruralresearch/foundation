Brinell Controls

Indenter: Hardened Steel Ball (Dia: 10 mm)

Brinell Formula

HBW = $\frac{2P}{\pi D (D - \sqrt{D^2 - d^2})}$

Where $P$ = Load (kgf), $D$ = Ball diameter (10 mm), $d$ = Indentation diameter (mm).

Rockwell Controls

Scale: Scale B (1/16" Ball) or Scale C (Diamond Cone)

Rockwell Principle

Measures permanent depth increase of penetration under minor (10 kgf) and major loads. Direct digital readout.

HR = $N - \frac{h}{0.002}$
