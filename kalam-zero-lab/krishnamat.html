Metrology Lab Manual, Formulas & Learning Outcomes

1. Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs)

  • Master Critical Dimension Scanning Electron Microscopy (CD-SEM) for sub-15nm gate line measurement.
  • Analyze Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM) tapping mode cantilever dynamics and RMS surface roughness ($R_a$).
  • Formulate spectroscopic ellipsometry equations for non-destructive gate oxide thickness measurement.
  • Calculate wafer sheet resistance ($R_s = \frac{\pi}{\ln 2} \frac{V}{I}$) and carrier doping concentration via 4-Point Probing.
  • Evaluate automated wafer laser scattering defect inspection and statistical yield correlation.

2. Fundamental Equations & Physics

• SEM De Broglie Wavelength: λ = h / √(2 · m_e · e · V)

• AFM Cantilever Spring Force: F = -k · Δz

• Spectroscopic Ellipsometry Ratio: ρ = tan(Ψ) · e^(iΔ)

• Four-Point Probe Sheet Resistance: R_s = (π / ln 2) · (V / I) ≈ 4.532 · (V / I)

3. Step-by-Step Guided Procedure

  1. Instrument Selection: Switch between CD-SEM, AFM, Ellipsometer, and 4-Point Probe in the interactive studio canvas.
  2. Parameter Control: Adjust Accelerating Voltage, Beam Current, Incident Angle, and Probe Spacing sliders in real time.
  3. Response Curve Observation: Monitor secondary electron intensity peaks, 3D surface roughness profiles, phase shifts ($\Psi, \Delta$), and I-V linear characteristics.
  4. Virtual Twin Inspection: Click the Virtual Twin docks (Device, Wafer Defect, Tool, Fab QC) to track real-time telemetry and process capability ($C_{pk}$).
