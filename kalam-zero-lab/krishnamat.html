Student Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the physical principles behind CD-SEM, Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), Ellipsometry, and Four-Point Probing.
  • Analyze the mathematical relationship between probe excitation parameters and semiconductor measurement resolution.
  • Evaluate how non-destructive metrology directly impacts statistical process control (SPC) and wafer yields in modern fabs.

Core Theoretical Formulas

1. SEM De Broglie Wavelength: λ = h / √(2 m e V)
2. AFM Cantilever Force: F = -k · Δz
3. Ellipsometry Ratio: ρ = tan(Ψ) · e^(iΔ)
4. Four-Point Sheet Resistance: R_s = (π / ln 2) · (V / I) ≈ 4.532 · (V / I)
