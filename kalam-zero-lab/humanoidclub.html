3. Scene 2: Testing Suite & The 5 Industrial Grippers

In Experiment 4, test and evaluate the 5 industrial end-effectors fitted to all hands across all three humanoids (Dr. Venu Gopal, Shantanu Bro, and the student's humanoid):

  • A) 2-Jaw Linear Parallel Gripper:
    Use Case: Rigid rectangular objects, burnt clay bricks, machine tool CNC loading, warehousing.
    Contact Mechanics: Squeezes parallel faces with normal clamping force $F_N \ge \frac{m \cdot g}{2 \mu_s} \times \text{SF} = 40.13\text{ N}$ ($P \approx 3.52\text{ kPa}$).
  • B) 13-DOF Anthropomorphic 5-Finger Bionic Hand:
    Use Case: Human-centric tasks, tool handling, delicate instrumentation, prosthetic robotics.
    Contact Mechanics: Multi-point friction cone equilibrium: $\sum \mathbf{F}_i = m(\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{g})$.
  • C) 3-Finger Centric Adaptive Gripper:
    Use Case: Cylindrical billets, pipes, spherical containers, orbital lathe turning stock.
    Contact Mechanics: Self-centering radial clamping: $F_{\text{rad}} = \frac{m \cdot g}{3 \mu_s \sin(\theta)}$.
  • D) Electro-Permanent Magnetic Gripper:
    Use Case: Ferrous steel sheet metal, automotive body panels, cast iron ingots.
    Contact Mechanics: Maxwell magnetic flux traction: $F_{\text{mag}} = \frac{B^2 \cdot A}{2 \mu_0}$. Non-ferrous clay bricks cannot be gripped.
  • E) Soft Compliant Silicone Fin-Ray Gripper:
    Use Case: Delicate agricultural produce (tomatoes, mangoes), eggs, fragile glass vials.
    Contact Mechanics: Bio-inspired passive envelope wrapping at low pressure ($P < 2.5\text{ kPa}$). Buckles under heavy bricks ($>3.0\text{ kg}$).
