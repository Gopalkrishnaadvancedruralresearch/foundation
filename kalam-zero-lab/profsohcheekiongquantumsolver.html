1. Material Specificity: Moisture vs. Metals

Porosity-based materials (Bamboo, Timber, Concrete) absorb moisture, altering structural stiffness and mass density. Metals (Aluminum, Steel) are impermeable; moisture has zero impact on their internal stress waves.
