1. Why is it called the "God Particle"?

Coined in 1993 by Nobel laureate Leon Lederman, the publisher chose the title "The God Particle" because the particle was so extraordinarily difficult to detect. In physics, its scientific name is the Higgs Boson. It represents the excitation of the all-pervading Higgs Field, which gives fundamental particles like quarks and electrons their rest mass.
