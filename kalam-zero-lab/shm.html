6 Specialized SHM Engineering Apps

Directly launch browser-native sensors, PZT impact test tools, and FFT spectrum engines.

phone_acc_gy.html

Phone Accelerometer & Gyroscope Logger

Real-time structural motion acquisition using smartphone IMUs. Captures 3-axis acceleration and angular velocity for dynamic structural response.

Launch Tool
pztimpacttest.html

PZT Impact Test Analyzer

High-speed transient stress wave acquisition and impact response evaluation using surface-bonded PZT transducers.

Core SHM Suite
shm_pzt.html

Electromechanical Impedance (EMI) PZT Tool

Electromechanical admittance signatures, structural baseline comparison, damage metric calculation (RMSD/MAPED), and crack assessment.

Launch Tool
pitch_catch_2phones.html

Dual-Phone Pitch-Catch Wave Suite

Synchronized multi-device active sensing setup. Phone 1 operates as the dynamic acoustic actuator; Phone 2 functions as the receiving sensor.

Launch Tool
pitch_catch_1phone.html

Single-Phone Pitch-Catch Ultrasonic Suite

Single-device pulse-echo ultrasonic excitation and echo reception for localized structural integrity and flaw monitoring.

FFT Analysis & Spectral Processing Suite

Fast Fourier Transform spectral engine. Extracts natural frequencies, dynamic resonance peaks, modal damping, and harmonic spectra.
