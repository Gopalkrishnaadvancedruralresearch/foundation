Lab Modules & Experiments

Select a metallurgical crystallography experiment to simulate X-ray interference and compute diffraction spectra:

  • Experiment 1: Verification of Bragg's Law ($n\lambda = 2d\sin\theta$) — Observe constructive interference across atomic lattice planes.
  • Experiment 2: Crystal System & Miller Indices — Evaluate diffraction peaks for FCC (Copper, Aluminum) and BCC (Iron) lattices.
  • Experiment 3: Crystallite Size Broadening (Scherrer Equation) — Analyze peak FWHM variations based on grain size ($L = \frac{K\lambda}{\beta\cos\theta}$).
