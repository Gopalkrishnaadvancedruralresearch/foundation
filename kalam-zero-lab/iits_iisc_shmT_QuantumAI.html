1. Why Quantum AI Architecture Must Differ for 1D, 2D, and 3D Mechanics

In physical electromechanical impedance (EMI) problems, applying a single generic quantum neural network across 1D beams, 2D plates, and 3D solids is physically invalid. The complexity of dynamic stress tensors and wave reflections differs fundamentally across dimensions, requiring tailor-made quantum ansatzes:

Mechanics Formulation Governing Wave Physics & Tensor Optimal Quantum AI Ansatz Hilbert Space Dimension Physical Decoupling Justification
1D Beam (Liang et al.) Uniaxial wave propagation ($T_2 = T_3 = 0$); single axial reflection path. 2-Qubit Variational Quantum Circuit (VQC) with Pauli-Z feature map. $\mathcal{H} = 2^2 = 4$ Basis States 4 basis states ($\Vert{}00\rangle, \Vert{}01\rangle, \Vert{}10\rangle, \Vert{}11\rangle$) are mathematically optimal to decouple 1D crack stiffness from thermal drift without barren plateaus.
2D Plate (Zhou / Bhalla & Soh) Biaxial plane stress ($T_3 = 0$) with Poisson ratio contraction ($\nu$) and radial modal reflections. 3-Qubit Quantum Convolutional Neural Network (QCNN) with Circular Entanglement ($CZ$). $\mathcal{H} = 2^3 = 8$ Basis States 8 basis states capture orthogonal wave vector interactions ($k_x, k_y$) and non-linear boundary reflections across four edges.
3D Continuum (Annamdas & Soh) Full 3D stress tensor ($T_1, T_2, T_3 \ne 0$), Rayleigh surface waves, and thickness dilatation mode (600–900 kHz). 4-Qubit Physics-Informed Quantum Neural Network (PI-QNN) with All-to-All Entanglement. $\mathcal{H} = 2^4 = 16$ Basis States 16 basis states are strictly required to resolve directional sum impedances across all 6 faces and decouple internal aggregate confinement from surface defects.

2. Mathematical Definition of Crack Geometry ($t_s = \text{Specimen Thickness}$)

In fracture mechanics and non-destructive evaluation, crack severity is non-dimensionalized with respect to the substrate thickness $t_s$:

  • Baseline ($S_0$): Pristine uncracked specimen ($d = 0.0\text{ mm}$, remaining thickness $100\%$).
  • Crack 1 ($S_1$): Incipient surface microcrack ($d = 0.10 \cdot t_s$, length $a = 2.0\text{ mm}$).
  • Crack 2 ($S_2$): Minor fatigue crack ($d = 0.30 \cdot t_s$, length $a = 5.0\text{ mm}$).
  • Crack 3 ($S_3$): Moderate structural fracture ($d = 0.50 \cdot t_s$, length $a = 10.0\text{ mm}$).
  • Crack 4 ($S_4$): Critical through-thickness crack ($d = 0.80 \cdot t_s$, length $a = 18.0\text{ mm}$).
