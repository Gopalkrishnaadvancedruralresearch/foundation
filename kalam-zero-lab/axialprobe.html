Material-Specific Ductile Fracture Behavior

Structural Steel

Exhibits classic symmetric **cup-and-cone** failure with a fibrous central region and $45^\circ$ shear lip rim.

Aluminum 6061

Displays sharper slanted shear lips and higher localized necking elongation due to FCC slip systems.

Cartridge Brass

Features smooth ductile cup formation with excellent reduction in area and fine dimpled rupture surfaces.
