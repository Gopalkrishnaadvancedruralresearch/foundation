How to Use the Probing & Pause Features

Set Dimensions & Setup

Enter your preferred initial length ($L_0$) and diameter ($D_0$), pick a material, and click Initialize Setup Rig to configure the testing frame.

Pause at Will

Run the automatic pull test or use the load slider manually. Hit **Pause** at any load level (e.g., right before yielding or during necking) to freeze the experiment.

Probe Various Locations

Click any point along the vertical rod to view how local strain spikes at the center due to necking while remaining uniform near the grips.
