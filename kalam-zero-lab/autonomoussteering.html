📖 Student HELP Guide & Step-by-Step Instructions

How to operate the experiment:

  1. Keep Chitti open at the bottom right to listen to verbal step-by-step guidance.
  2. Scroll down to the Control Panel on the left side of the workbench.
  3. Adjust the Vehicle Velocity ($v$) and Lookahead Distance ($L_d$) sliders to test different driving conditions.

  5. Scroll to the very bottom to test your knowledge with the assessment quiz!

📚 What Exactly Do Students Learn Here? (Engineering Theory)

This experiment introduces core principles of autonomous vehicle control:

  • Kinematic Bicycle Model: Simplifying complex multi-wheel vehicle steering physics into front-wheel steering and rear-wheel axis geometry.
  • Lookahead Distance ($L_d$): The predictive distance ahead on the path used to calculate heading errors and smooth out steering actuation.
  • Error Minimization: Real-time correction of cross-track deviations to prevent vehicle drift off the reference trajectory.
