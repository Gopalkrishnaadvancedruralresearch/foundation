Mathematical Derivation & Equations

Applying Euler-Lagrange equations with generalized coordinates q = [x, θ]ᵀ:

(M + m)ẍ - mlθ̈ cos θ + mlθ̇² sin θ + bẋ = u
mlẍ cos θ + I_rod θ̈ - mgl sin θ + b_θ θ̇ = 0

Linearization around the unstable equilibrium (θ = 0, x = 0) yields the state-space form ẋ = Ax + Bu. The Riccati equation minimizes quadratic cost:

J = ∫ [xᵀ Q x + uᵀ R u] dt ⟹ u = -K x
