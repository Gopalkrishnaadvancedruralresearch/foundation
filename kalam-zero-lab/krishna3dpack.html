What Changed? Physics & Observation

Changing from Planar to GAA Nanosheet provides gate electrostatic control from all 4 sides, reducing Subthreshold Swing (SS) towards the theoretical limit of ~60 mV/dec and suppressing Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL).

Lab Guide & Student Learning Outcomes

How to Perform Experiments:

  1. Select Visual Mode: Switch between Transistor Architecture, 2.5D Interposer, and 3D Thermal Heatmap at the canvas top.
  2. Interactive Steps 1-5: Adjust parameters like Gate Length, Micro-bump Pitch, TSV Aspect Ratio, and Cooling Efficiency.
  3. Observe Real-Time Graphs: Monitor subthreshold swing, insertion loss (S21), stress contours, and HBM memory bandwidth.
  4. Inspect Virtual Twins: Open the Virtual Twin buttons at the top to inspect device leakage, substrate crosstalk, and Known Good Die (KGD) fab economics.
  5. Download Reference Materials: Access chip.pdf for chiplet design rules and physical layout guidelines.

Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs):

  • Understand sub-2nm Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet physics and short-channel effect suppression.
  • Analyze high-density signal integrity and insertion loss across 2.5D silicon interposers.
  • Evaluate mechanical stress and void defect rates in Through-Silicon Vias (TSVs) during direct hybrid bonding.
  • Calculate High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM3) throughput as a function of bus width and stack height.
  • Formulate dynamic thermal management strategies for high-density 3D stacked system-in-package architectures.
