🔍 Component Study Hub

Let's study what physical components look like and how they function together in our lab:

1. Microcontroller Boards (Arduino Uno, Mega, Nano, ESP32)

Appearance: Compact printed circuit boards with microchips, digital/analog pins, and USB ports.

Function: Acts as the programmable intelligence and brain of your robot.

2. L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver

Appearance: Red/blue board with a large aluminum heatsink and screw terminal blocks.

Function: Regulates high current and voltage from the battery to drive DC motors forward and backward.

3. 12V Geared DC Center-Shaft Motor

Appearance: Metal cylindrical motor attached to a metal gearbox with an offset shaft.

Function: Converts electrical energy into mechanical torque for wheel rotation.

4. 11.1V 3S LiPo Battery Pack

Appearance: Wrapped black block with heavy silicone discharge wires and XT60 connector.

Function: Provides stable high-discharge current to power motors and electronics.
