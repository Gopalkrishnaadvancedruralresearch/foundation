📖 GARRF Educational Help & Theory Guide: Road Estimation Engine

1. Road Classifications, Lane Configurations & IRC Geometries

Under Indian Roads Congress (IRC) standards, highway geometry dictates the cross-sectional width and material volumes:

  • 2-Lane Two-Way: Total carriage width 7.0m + 2.5m shoulders each side. Designed for moderate rural/state traffic.
  • 4-Lane Divided: Two independent carriageways (7.5m each) separated by a central median (1.5m to 4.5m).
  • 6-Lane Expressway: High-speed arterial corridor with 3 lanes per direction (11.25m carriage per side) + paved shoulders.
  • 8-Lane High-Capacity: Ultra-wide corridors (4 lanes per direction) handling heavy inter-state commercial freight.

2. Pavement Types & Required Civil Engineering Materials

Pavement Type Structural Layers Required Materials
Flexible Pavement (Bituminous) Subgrade → Granular Sub-Base (GSB) → Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) → Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) → Bituminous Concrete (BC) Crushed stone aggregates, quarry dust, bitumen binder (VG-30/VG-40), granular soil. (~1,970 MT per lane-km per meter width).
Rigid Pavement (Concrete / PQC) Subgrade → Granular Sub-Base → Dry Lean Concrete (DLC) → Pavement Quality Concrete (PQC Slab) Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC 53), coarse/fine river sand, high-strength aggregates, steel dowel and tie bars. (~1,900 MT per lane-km).

3. Theory Behind the 15 Concurrent Algorithms

The estimation studio benchmarks three distinct computational paradigms simultaneously:

🤖 Classical Machine Learning (Models 1–7)

Utilizes historical IRC data regression trees, multi-layer perceptrons with gradient descent, support vector regressors (SVR), ridge/lasso penalization, XGBoost, and KNN to fit empirical material and cost coefficients.

🧬 Evolutionary & Heuristic Optimizers (Models 8–10)

Deep Neural Networks (DNN), Genetic Algorithms (GA mimicking natural selection and genetic crossover for cost reduction), and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO simulating flocking behavior to locate optimal multi-variable cost balance).

⚛️ Quantum & Quantum-Inspired Models (Models 11–15)

Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE), Quantum Annealing (QUBO energy formulation), Quantum Neural Networks (QNN), and Grover-Accelerated Search. These map road layer optimization into qubit energy states.

4. Why Quantum Algorithms Require Zero Safety Buffer

A critical observation in the comparative dashboard is that Quantum models show 0.0% Exact (QUBO) buffer, whereas classical models add positive safety margins (+1.5% to +5%):

  • Elimination of Local Minima: Classical heuristics search parameter spaces iteratively and can get trapped in local suboptimal valleys, requiring engineers to add contingency safety buffers.
  • Superposition & Quantum Tunneling: Quantum algorithms evaluate all possible material thickness combinations simultaneously across multidimensional Hilbert space.
  • Exact Mathematical Global Optimum: When the quantum state collapses, it yields the exact global minimum energy state. Because the mathematical output is rigorous and free of heuristic approximation slacks, no artificial padding or buffer is necessary.
