The EMI technique utilizes bonded Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT) ceramic transducers driven at high ultrasonic frequencies (20 kHz to 200 kHz). Through direct and converse piezoelectric coupling, the electrical admittance Y(ω) = G(ω) + jB(ω) across the PZT patch is coupled directly to the local mechanical impedance of the robot's skeletal joint:

Y(ω) = jω · (w·l / h) · [ε33T - d312 · YE · (Za / (Zs + Za))]

Any crack, bolt loosening, or structural micro-fissure directly modifies the structural impedance Zs, producing immediate peak left-shifts, amplitude damping, and anti-resonance distortion in the conductance spectrum Re(Y).
