1. Theory & Mathematical Foundations

Piezoelectric Guided Waves & Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)
Guided waves are elastic stress waves bounded by structural geometries such as thin metallic or composite plates. Surface-bonded Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT) transducers convert electrical excitation into high-frequency mechanical strain via the inverse piezoelectric effect, actuating ultrasonic Lamb waves throughout the plate structure.

1.1 Fundamental Lamb Wave Modes

Ultrasonic Lamb waves propagate in thin plates through two primary dispersive modes:

  • Symmetric ($S_0$ Mode): Extensional in-plane motion dominating high frequencies with rapid group velocity propagation.
  • Antisymmetric ($A_0$ Mode): Flexural out-of-plane displacement, exhibiting extreme sensitivity to localized structural discontinuities, surface cracks, corrosion pits, and debonding.

1.2 Time-of-Flight (ToF) & Crack Geometry Physics

For a direct path between Actuator ($A$) and Sensor ($S$) across distance $d_{AS}$, the baseline Time-of-Flight ($ToF_{\text{direct}}$) is defined as:

$$ToF_{\text{direct}} = \frac{d_{AS}}{c_g} = \frac{\sqrt{(X_S - X_A)^2 + (Y_S - Y_A)^2}}{c_g}$$

When a Surface Crack with length $\ell_c$ and inclination angle $\theta_c$ is positioned at center $(X_D, Y_D)$ (measured from the left edge $X_0=0$ and bottom $Y_0=0$), the scattered echo path length $d_{\text{scatter}}$ is given by:

$$d_{\text{scatter}} = d_{AD} + d_{DS} = \sqrt{(X_D - X_A)^2 + (Y_D - Y_A)^2} + \sqrt{(X_S - X_D)^2 + (Y_S - Y_D)^2}$$
$$ToF_{\text{scatter}} = \frac{d_{\text{scatter}}}{c_g} \quad \Bigg| \quad \Delta t = ToF_{\text{scatter}} - ToF_{\text{direct}}$$

The scattered wave amplitude $A_{\text{scatter}}$ scales proportionally with the relative crack length $\ell_c$:

$$A_{\text{scatter}} \propto A_0 \cdot \left( \frac{\ell_c}{\lambda} \right) \sin(\theta_c)$$
