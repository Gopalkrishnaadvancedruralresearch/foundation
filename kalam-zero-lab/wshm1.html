SECTION 5

How to Find Damage Location Using Time-of-Flight (ToF)

Step A: Record Healthy Baseline Signal

First, actuate a clean plate without any damage. You will observe a single primary wave packet arriving at the Sensor at time t_direct. This is your Healthy Baseline Signal.

Step B: Observe Damaged Signal Behavior

When structural damage occurs, a portion of the wave strikes the damage boundary and bounces back. The Sensor catches two distinct pulses:

1. Direct Wave: Arrives directly from Actuator → Sensor (time: t_direct).
2. Scattered Echo: Travels along Actuator → Damage → Sensor (time: t_echo).

Step C: Calculate Damage Distance (Principle)

Using the wave speed inside the aluminum plate (5.4 km/s or 5400 m/s), calculate the total travel distance:

Total Distance = Wave Speed × t_echo

By measuring echo arrival times across multiple sensor paths, the exact (X, Y) position of the crack is calculated on the plate.

SECTION 6

SHM Laboratories Infrastructure in India

Where are these physical laboratories available in India?

Physical Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) testbeds and wave propagation research facilities are active across Indian higher educational institutions, including premier technical institutes (various IITs and IISc), premier national aerospace/structural research centers (CSIR laboratories), and select engineering universities.
