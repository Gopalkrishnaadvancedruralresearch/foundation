Visual Laboratory Suite for SemiConductor Design and Development

Dedicated to the Viksit Bharat Mission 2047, the Gopalkrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) presents a complete 8-module virtual laboratory suite. Designed with a soft, eye-soothing light blue background and crisp gray cards for extended student research, this suite provides complete experiential learning from ISO cleanrooms to 3D packaging and custom ASIC chip design.
