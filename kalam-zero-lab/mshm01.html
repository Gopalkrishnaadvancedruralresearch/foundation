Experiment SHM-01

Smartphone Accelerometer Free-Vibration & Damping Analysis

Welcome to GARRF's browser-native structural health monitoring lab. This experiment uses your smartphone’s native internal MEMS accelerometer as a live testing sensor. By placing your phone on a physical beam model, students can study free-vibration decay curves, structural damping, and natural frequencies without expensive hardware.

📱 Notice: This experiment requires mobile hardware sensors. Please open this web link directly on your smartphone browser to run the test.
