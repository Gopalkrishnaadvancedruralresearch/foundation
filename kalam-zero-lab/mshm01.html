Experiment SHM-01

Smartphone Accelerometer Free-Vibration & Damping Analysis

Welcome to GARRF's browser-native structural health monitoring virtual laboratory. This experiment converts your mobile device into a precision testing accelerometer to analyze structural dynamics without expensive instrumentation.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Free Vibration & Natural Frequency: How structures oscillate freely after an initial impact.
  • Structural Damping: How energy dissipates over time through a decaying amplitude wave.
  • data-driven telemetry: Capturing real-world acceleration data for engineering computations and lab reports.

🔬 Experimental Nature & Procedure

In structural engineering, accelerometers are mounted on beams or bridges to measure dynamic response. In this zero-cost frugal experiment:

How to Mount Your Phone on the Structure:
  1. Setup your physical lab beam model (cantilever or simply supported beam).
  2. Place your smartphone flat on top of the beam structure near the free end.
  3. Ensure the phone is stable so it moves strictly with the beam's deflection.
