11. CHITTI — Student → Senior Engineering Path

CHITTI is integrated into the workflow so that a student does not remain at the "calculator" level. The system gradually moves the learner toward engineering reasoning, trade-offs, verification and design review.

CHITTI LEVEL 1

Student Engineering Assistant

Helps the student understand:

  • What should I calculate?
  • Which component should I select?
  • Why is this resistor required?
  • Why does the motor need a driver?
  • What happens if voltage is wrong?
  • How does battery capacity affect runtime?
CHITTI SENIOR

Senior Engineering Reasoning

Moves beyond simple answers and asks:

  • What are the design assumptions?
  • Where is the engineering margin?
  • What is the failure mode?
  • What are the competing design choices?
  • What should be measured experimentally?
  • Can the design be justified technically?
