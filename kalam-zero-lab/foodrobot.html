Student Handbook: Hyderabad Autonomous Robotics

Step-by-step curriculum to implement 'Garrf-Khaana Boy' in Telangana

Step 1: High-Definition (HD) Vector Mapping

Robots don't just rely on standard consumer GPS. Students use SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) to map every curb, tree, curb ramp, and fire hydrant across HITEC City and DLF Food Street with centimeter accuracy.

Step 2: Dynamic Obstacle Costmaps (A* & Dijkstra Algorithm)

When an auto-rickshaw stops abruptly or IT crowd clusters form during lunch hour, Garrf-Khaana Boy's costmap creates virtual high-cost zones, continuously computing alternate spline paths in real-time.

Step 3: Multi-Stop Travelling Salesperson Heuristic

In multi-drop runs (e.g., collecting Osmania biscuits from Charminar Chai Co., then dropping to T-Hub, Cyber Pearl, and Mindspace), the backend optimizer solves a constrained TSP (Travelling Salesperson Problem) minimizing battery draw and delivery delays.

Step 4: Real-World Testing Sandbox in Telangana

T-Hub and Telangana AI Mission (T-AIM) provide regulatory sandboxes for testing delivery robots inside enclosed tech parks before street-level permits are expanded to broader GHMC roads.
