📖 EMI Aerospace SHM Educational & Conceptual Lab Guide

1. Intelligent Pedagogical Concept: PZT Array Multiplexing

In structural health monitoring of aircraft structures, multiple Piezoelectric Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT) patches are attached across critical spars, frames, and skins. This simulator conceptualizes PZT Multiplexing, where an array of transducers is monitored through a single impedance analyzer channel using switching logic.

2. Cumulative Admittance Formulation

The electrical admittance $Y(\omega) = G(\omega) + j B(\omega)$ measured at any active actuator represents the cumulative electro-mechanical coupling of the entire structure and nearby bonded PZT sensors.

📚 Academic Reference for Conceptual Study:
"The electromechanical admittance measured across multiplexed PZT networks reflects the cumulative coupled dynamic interaction between individual piezo-actuators and the host structural modes."
— Annamdas, V. G. M., & Soh, C. K. (2007). "Electromechanical Impedance Modeling and PZT Multiplexing in Structural Health Monitoring". Refer to literature for full analytical derivation.

3. Baseline vs. Damage Signatures: Peak Shifts & RMSD Dynamics

Students should closely observe how damage influences electromechanical signatures:

  • Frequency Shift (Mode Shift): Structural stiffness reduction (e.g., crack propagation or fastener loosening) causes high-frequency resonant peaks in $G(\omega)$ and $B(\omega)$ to shift downward (leftward).
  • Peak Damping & Amplitude Alteration: Local structural damping reduces peak amplitudes or creates new micro-resonance peaks.
  • Root Mean Square Deviation (RMSD) Metric: RMSD quantifies signature deviation from healthy baseline: $$\text{RMSD} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( G_i^d - G_i^h \right)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( G_i^h \right)^2}} \times 100\%$$
⚠️ Important Signal Interpretation Note for Students:
A higher RMSD value generally indicates significant structural alteration, but RMSD does NOT always increase monotonically with severity. At high damage levels or specific anti-resonance regions, signature cancellations can occur. RMSD study must always be tied together with frequency-shift and modal analysis to correctly diagnose damage severity.
