PIC & Quantum Lab Guide & Learning Outcomes

How to Perform Experiments:

  1. Select View Mode: Toggle between 2D Layout, 3D Waveguide Cross-Section, and Quantum Gate Grid at the top canvas.
  2. Step-by-Step Workflow: Navigate through Steps 1 to 5 (Silicon Waveguide, Ring Resonator, MZI, Qubit Control, Metrology).
  3. Tune Parameters: Adjust real-time sliders (waveguide dimensions, phase shift, microwave frequency, cryo temperature).
  4. Observe Live Graphs: Monitor response spectrums, extinction ratios, and Rabi oscillation plots updated instantly.
  5. Virtual Twins Telemetry: Open Virtual Twin docks to inspect real-time wafer die yields and cryogenic refrigerator logs.

Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs):

  • Master single-mode optical waveguide geometry cutoff conditions and propagation loss mechanics.
  • Understand optical ring resonator resonance condition ($\lambda_0 = \frac{2\pi R n_{eff}}{m}$) and Quality factor ($Q$).
  • Analyze Electro-Optic phase modulation inside Mach-Zehnder Interferometers (MZI).
  • Comprehend Transmon Qubit energy state manipulation on the Bloch Sphere via resonant microwave pulses.
  • Evaluate Single Photon Detector (SNSPD) efficiency and quantum state fidelity metrics.
