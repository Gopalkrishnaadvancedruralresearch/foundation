SECTION 1

1. Theory & Mathematical Foundations

Guided Stress Waves in Bounded Elastic Media:
Guided waves represent traction-free boundary-condition solutions to elastodynamic Navier equations within plate geometries. Surface-bonded Lead Zirconate Titanate ($PZT$) transducers actuate ultrasonic Lamb modes through localized dynamic surface tractions.

1.1 Rayleigh–Lamb Characteristic Equations

For an isotropic elastic plate of total thickness $d=2h$, density $\rho$, and Lamé parameters $\lambda,\mu$:

$$\text{Symmetric Mode }(S):\quad \frac{\tan(qh)}{\tan(ph)} =-\frac{4k^2pq}{(k^2-q^2)^2}$$
$$\text{Antisymmetric Mode }(A):\quad \frac{\tan(qh)}{\tan(ph)} =-\frac{(k^2-q^2)^2}{4k^2pq}$$

where $p^2=\frac{\omega^2}{c_L^2}-k^2$, $q^2=\frac{\omega^2}{c_T^2}-k^2$, $c_L=\sqrt{(\lambda+2\mu)/\rho}$ and $c_T=\sqrt{\mu/\rho}$.

💡 Arrival physics: phase velocity $c_p$ describes phase-front propagation. For a narrowband packet, envelope/energy arrival is primarily associated with group velocity $c_g$. In real measurements, packet picking also depends on bandwidth, mode content, thresholding and signal processing.

6. Inverse Problem Formulation & ARJUN Assistant

Inverse Kinematic Formulation:
A single pitch-catch pair constrains damage location to an ellipse with actuator and sensor as foci.

$$\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{a^2}+\frac{(y-y_0)^2}{b^2}=1, \qquad 2a=d_{\mathrm{scatter}}=c_g\,ToF_{\mathrm{scatter}}$$

Multiple independent, non-collinear paths reduce spatial ambiguity. The estimated point should be reported separately from the known simulated damage coordinate.

Academic honesty: all measurements in this browser laboratory are simulated. They are not substitutes for experimental PZT/DAQ measurements or validated FEM results.

STUDENT GUIDE

7. Two-Way Teaching: How Each Mode Works

🔴 STRICT PROFESSOR — for disciplined/protocol-driven students

Prof. Manohar checks the sequence. Trial 1 must be Healthy. He asks the student to justify dispersion, ToF and velocity errors, discourages unsupported conclusions, and requires completion of the protocol before declaring the experiment complete.

🔵 INTERACTIVE PROFESSOR — for exploratory/concept-driven students

Prof. Manohar uses questions, hints and explanations. Students may explore parameters, compare damage conditions, repeat observations and discuss why the waveform or localization changes.

Recommended learning sequence: Theory → dispersion operating point → healthy baseline → damage introduction → A-scan/DSP → ToF → group velocity → multi-path localization → error analysis → conclusion.
