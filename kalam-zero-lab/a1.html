10. 🧠 Student Engineering Questions

Question 1

Why should a DC motor normally not be connected directly to an Arduino GPIO pin?

A motor can require substantially more current than a microcontroller GPIO is designed to provide. A motor driver creates the appropriate power path while the Arduino supplies control signals.

Question 2

The battery voltage is correct, but the robot resets whenever both motors start. What should you investigate?

Investigate startup/stall current, battery discharge capability, wiring resistance, connectors, grounding and voltage drop.

Question 3

What turns an ordinary robot into an intelligent robot?

Intelligence develops through sensing, state estimation, decision-making, feedback and eventually learning from data.
