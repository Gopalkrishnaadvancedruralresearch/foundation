❓ Charan Das Desktop Companion Guide

📱 24/7 Desk Buddy Setup Guide

  1. Deploy & Launch: Save file as index.html. Host via GitHub Pages, local server, or open in Chrome.
  2. Enable Stay Awake (Android): Go to Settings → About Phone, tap Build Number 7 times. Then in Developer Options, turn on "Stay Awake" so the display stays permanently on while charging.
  3. Battery Longevity: In Settings → Battery, enable "Protect Battery" (limits charging to 80–85% to protect battery health during 24/7 docking).
  4. Permissions & Kiosk: Tap anywhere on the screen once loaded to grant Camera and Microphone permissions. Tap Fullscreen for a distraction-free display.
  5. Desktop Docking: Place phone on an angled desk stand connected to USB power. Charan Das will continuously track your eyes and listen to your voice.
