Theoretical Overview & Engineering Significance
Mohr's Circle Transformation: A graphical representation of the transformation of 2D stress tensors. It allows engineers to easily visualize principal stresses and maximum shear stresses without complex manual tensor algebra.
Beam Sign Conventions: Shear force is positive when tending to shear the segment clockwise. Bending moment is positive when causing tension on the bottom fibers (sagging).
