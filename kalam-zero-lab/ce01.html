Experiment Objective & Theoretical Framework

Objective: To study the static, dynamic, and kinematic response of prismatic structural beams under 8 distinct loading regimes, verifying Euler-Bernoulli bending assumptions, shear force distributions, and maximum deflections without physical lab constraints.

Governing Equations: The elastic deflection curve $v(x)$ is governed by the fourth-order differential equation $\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\left(EI \frac{d^2v}{dx^2}\right) = q(x)$, where $EI$ represents the flexural rigidity, and $q(x)$ is the distributed load function. Integration yields real-time reactions $R_A, R_B$, internal shear forces $V(x)$, and bending moments $M(x)$.

Virtual Laboratory Step-by-Step Procedure
  1. Initialize Workspace: Choose your desired experiment number (1 through 8) from the dropdown control panel.
  2. Configure Parameters: Adjust the Load Magnitude ($P$), Beam Span ($L$), and Flexural Rigidity ($EI$) using the interactive sliders.
  3. Analyze Visual Plots: Observe the real-time deflection profile, Shear Force Diagram (SFD), and Bending Moment Diagram (BMD) rendered on the canvas.
  4. Record Analytical Metrics: Note down reactions ($R_A, R_B$), peak bending moments ($M_{max}$), and maximum deflection ($\delta_{max}$) for your lab report sheet.
  5. Verify with Quiz: Complete the student evaluation quiz below to validate your conceptual understanding before concluding the session.
