What is the Kalam Zero Research Funding Lab?

A new model for engineering education and research: experiment first, learn deeply and innovate freely.

From Zero Funding to Zero Barriers

The Kalam Zero Research Funding Lab is an initiative of the GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF).

It is designed around a simple principle: students should not have to wait for expensive laboratories, institutional approvals or large research grants to begin learning how engineering systems actually work.

Through interactive virtual experiments, students can observe, calculate, modify parameters, test hypotheses and develop engineering intuition.

The long-term vision is to develop a large catalogue of interactive experiments covering multiple engineering disciplines.

Our Philosophy

Experimentation should be accessible to everyone.

A student with curiosity and an internet connection should be able to explore engineering concepts, conduct virtual experiments and develop ideas.

The laboratory therefore aims to reduce the dependence on expensive physical infrastructure while complementing, rather than replacing, real-world laboratory education.
