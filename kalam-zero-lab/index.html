What is the Kalam Zero Research Funding Lab?

A new model for engineering education and research: experiment first, learn deeply and innovate freely.

The Kalam Zero Research Funding Lab is an initiative of the GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF).

Students should not have to wait for expensive laboratories, institutional approvals or large research grants to begin learning how engineering systems actually work.

Through interactive virtual experiments, students can observe, calculate, modify parameters, test hypotheses and develop engineering intuition.

“Experimentation should be accessible to everyone.”
CONCEPT→ EXPERIMENT→ SIMULATION→ DESIGN→ PROTOTYPE→ INNOVATION

From Zero Funding to Zero Barriers

The laboratory reduces dependence on expensive physical infrastructure while complementing — not replacing — real-world laboratory education.

A student with curiosity and an internet connection can explore engineering concepts, conduct virtual experiments and develop ideas.

Long-term vision: a large catalogue of interactive experiments covering multiple engineering disciplines.
