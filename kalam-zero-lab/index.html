HOW TO USE KALAM ZERO LAB

You do not need to be an expert to begin. Choose an experiment, understand the objective, operate the virtual setup, observe the result and learn why it happens.

🔬 WHAT IS A VIRTUAL EXPERIMENT?

A virtual experiment is an interactive digital laboratory experience that allows you to explore engineering concepts through a computer or mobile device. You can interact with parameters, observe changes, collect results and develop engineering understanding without requiring physical laboratory equipment at every step.

04
📊

Watch the system response, measurements and graphs.

05
💡

Ask why the result changed and connect it with theory.
