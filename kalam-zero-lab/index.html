Frugal Innovation in Engineering

Providing zero-funding research tools, open-source mobile diagnostics, and advanced structural health monitoring systems to engineering colleges nationwide.

Educational Research Pathways

1

Mobile Digital Utilities

Start by converting everyday smartphones into powerful laboratory equipment using MIT App Inventor. Students learn to capture and map environmental data utilizing built-in device sensors.

2

Open-Source Robotics

Progress to microcontroller platforms. Students build logic for Arduino-driven robotics, integrating ultrasonic and force-sensing resistors to evaluate structural wear and tear.

3

Advanced Diagnostics

Culminate in professional-grade Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) utilizing Piezoelectric (PZT) sensors and the Duo-System architecture for deep material analysis.

The Virtual Laboratory

Exp 01: Magnetic Field Mapper
MIT App Inventor

A zero-cost digital utility app allowing students to measure and map local magnetic field variations around structural steel to detect hidden anomalies.

Exp 02: Smart Inclinometer
Mobile Sensor Array

Utilizing smartphone gyroscopes and accelerometers to measure structural tilt and vibration, simulating expensive laboratory-grade inclinometers.

Exp 03: Automated Wear Detection
Arduino Robotics

Design sensor integration and motor controls to deploy a small-scale robotic crawler that evaluates track and rail wear-and-tear using acoustic feedback.
