About the Zero Research Funding Initiative

Traditional engineering education often stalls due to high setup costs, expensive instrumentation, and restricted laboratory access. The Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Zero Research Funding Lab breaks down these barriers entirely by offering web-based, zero-cost digital simulation laboratories.

Designed under the Gopalkrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF), these tools empower students across India and globally to simulate real-world phenomena ranging from structural health monitoring to advanced electromechanical systems.
