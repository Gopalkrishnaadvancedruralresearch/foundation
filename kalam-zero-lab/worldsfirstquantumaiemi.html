MORE THAN TWO DECADES OF RESEARCH

The Work Behind Annamdas 3D

The 3D Quantum-AI EMI–PZT environment grows from more than two decades of research in Electromechanical Impedance, PZT sensing, structural interaction and Structural Health Monitoring.

My research journey has included three-dimensional EMI modelling, PZT–structure interaction, experimental investigation and the development of computational approaches for advanced SHM research.

The present work extends that research direction into a new 3D Quantum-AI computational environment, allowing researchers to explore advanced engineering problems virtually.

RESEARCH LINEAGE

From 3D EMI to 3D Quantum-AI

A compact view of the research evolution.

2007
Three-Dimensional EMI Model
Development of a three-dimensional electromechanical impedance formulation and directional sum impedance concept.
2007
EMI–PZT Structural Research
Research into the interaction between PZT sensing, structures and EMI response.
2008
Multiple-PZT / Structure Interaction
Further investigation of multiple PZT patches, structural interaction and the influence of PZT mass and associated parameters.
2026
3D Quantum-AI EMI–PZT
Extension of the research direction into an advanced virtual computational environment combining 3D EMI–PZT concepts with Quantum-AI methods.
