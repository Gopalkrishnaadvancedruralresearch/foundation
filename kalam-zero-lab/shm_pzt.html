WHY THIS LAB?

Advanced Research. Open Access.

Structural Health Monitoring using Piezoelectric Transducers is an advanced engineering and research field involving sensors, smart materials, signal processing, vibration, wave propagation and artificial intelligence.

Bringing advanced PZT education to rural students.

Technologies such as PZT-based Structural Health Monitoring are commonly explored in advanced international university laboratories and research environments. Several leading Indian institutions, including IITs, are also active in PZT and Structural Health Monitoring research.

But access to specialised laboratory equipment, sensors, instrumentation and research facilities can be difficult for many students — particularly those studying in rural and resource-constrained engineering institutions.

GARRF is creating this virtual laboratory to make the fundamental concepts accessible, interactive and free for students.

The objective is not to replace a physical research laboratory. Instead, this virtual environment gives students an opportunity to understand the principles, perform simulated experiments and develop the confidence to work with real SHM systems later.

LEARN THE COMPLETE PROCESS

From PZT Excitation
to Damage Identification

The virtual experiment will allow students to visually follow the complete Structural Health Monitoring chain.

PZT Excitation

Electrical excitation is applied to the PZT.

Structural Waves

Mechanical waves propagate through the structure.

Damage Interaction

Cracks and stiffness changes alter wave behaviour.

PZT Sensing

The PZT senses the structural response.

Signal Acquisition

Response data is captured for analysis.

Frequency Analysis

FFT reveals changes in the frequency spectrum.

Damage Identification

Indicators and AI can assist in identifying damage.
