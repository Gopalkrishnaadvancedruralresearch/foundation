🎓 GARRF AI Acharya — 100% Offline & Private Application:

🔒 Local Data Security: This application runs entirely within your web browser. Your uploaded textbooks, scanned PDFs, handwritten notes, journals, and research papers never leave your device, are never uploaded anywhere, and remain 100% secure.

👁️ Scanned & Handwritten Notes (OCR): If you upload scanned documents or handwritten pages, AI Acharya automatically runs local Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to read the handwriting and images directly!

🏷️ Customizing Course Titles: Type any subject or book name in the "Course Name / Research Subject" input box to retitle your study session. Settings and titles are saved automatically locally.

🗣️ Voice Selection & Natural Audio: Select your preferred speaker voice (Indian, US, or UK English, male or female) under the speech settings dropdown.

▶️ Continue Reading Control: Click "▶ Continue Reading" after each statement to have AI Acharya read step-by-step with the caricature following along!
