GARRF Virtual Lab Guide & Learning Outcomes

Welcome to KRISHNA Virtual Chip Lab

This virtual lab simulates end-to-end VLSI design and semiconductor manufacturing physics developed for researchers and students across India.

Step-by-Step Learning Guide:

  1. Design Phase: Select process nodes (from 28nm down to 3nm GAAFET) to observe transistor density scaling and gate delay dynamics on the central KRISHNA core.
  2. Lithography: Adjust Rayleigh formula parameters ($\text{CD} = k_1 \frac{\lambda}{\text{NA}}$) using EUV (13.5nm) vs DUV sources.
  3. Deposition & Etching: Plasma RIE anisotropy controls, aspect ratio calculation, and film thickness deposition.
  4. Doping & Metrology: Ion implantation dose/energy calculations and ellipsometry measurement logs.
  5. Virtual Twins: Explore the 4 interactive modals to inspect physical Equipment plasma, 300mm Wafer Die Maps, Device TDP Curves, and Fab OEE economics.
