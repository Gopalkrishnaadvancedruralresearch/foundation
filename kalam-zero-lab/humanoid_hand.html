Humanoid Subsystem[cite: 2]
What It Is:[cite: 2]

Remote tendon winch spools placed within the forearm skeletal frame, connected to an anthropomorphic 5-finger gripper mounted on the right arm of the humanoid torso[cite: 2].

Why Humanoids Use It:[cite: 2]

Reduces distal mass on the wrist joint. Heavy motors are placed near the forearm and torso, keeping arm inertia low and enabling rapid picking dynamics[cite: 2].

Engineering Specs / DIY Recommendation:[cite: 2]

Dyneema 0.45mm braid (breaking strength > 450N). Actuation via high-torque coreless BLDCs with AS5048A 14-bit magnetic absolute encoders[cite: 2].
