1. Theory & Mathematical Foundations

Piezoelectric Guided Waves & Structural Health Monitoring
Guided waves are elastic stress waves bounded by structural geometries such as thin metallic or composite plates. Surface-bonded Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT) transducers convert electrical excitation into high-frequency mechanical strain via the inverse piezoelectric effect, actuating ultrasonic Lamb waves throughout the plate structure.

1.1 Fundamental Lamb Wave Modes ($S_0$ & $A_0$)

Ultrasonic Lamb waves propagate in thin plates through two primary dispersive modes across the plate cross-section ($x$-$y$ plane):

  • Symmetric ($S_0$ Mode): Extensional in-plane motion dominating high frequencies with rapid group velocity propagation. Upper and lower surfaces move symmetrically in opposite vertical directions relative to the neutral plane[cite: 2].
  • Antisymmetric ($A_0$ Mode): Flexural out-of-plane displacement. Upper and lower surfaces move in phase in the same vertical direction, exhibiting extreme sensitivity to surface cracks, corrosion, and debonding[cite: 2].

Symmetric ($S_0$) Extensional Mode in $x$-$y$ Plane[cite: 2]

In-plane stretching/compression symmetric about neutral axis $y=0$[cite: 2].

Antisymmetric ($A_0$) Flexural Bending Mode in $x$-$y$ Plane[cite: 2]

Out-of-plane flexural bending across plate thickness in $x$-$y$ plane[cite: 2].

💡 Visualization Note:
In the $S_0$ mode, notice how the top and bottom surfaces push and pull symmetrically about the center line (extensional motion in $x$). In contrast, in the $A_0$ mode, both surfaces bend together up and down in unison along the $y$-axis (flexural bending).

1.2 Time-of-Flight (ToF) & Dynamic Scattering Physics[cite: 2]

For a direct path between Actuator ($A$) and Sensor ($S$) across distance $d_{AS}$, the direct wave Time-of-Flight ($ToF_{\text{direct}}$) is defined as[cite: 2]:

$$ToF_{\text{direct}} = \frac{d_{AS}}{c_g} = \frac{\sqrt{(X_S - X_A)^2 + (Y_S - Y_A)^2}}{c_g}$$

When a structural flaw with length $\ell_c$ and angle $\theta_c$ is positioned at center $(X_D, Y_D)$ (measured from the left edge $X_0=0$ and bottom $Y_0=0$), the scattered echo path length $d_{\text{scatter}}$ is given by[cite: 2]:

$$d_{\text{scatter}} = d_{AD} + d_{DS} = \sqrt{(X_D - X_A)^2 + (Y_D - Y_A)^2} + \sqrt{(X_S - X_D)^2 + (Y_S - Y_D)^2}$$
$$ToF_{\text{scatter}} = \frac{d_{\text{scatter}}}{c_g}$$
