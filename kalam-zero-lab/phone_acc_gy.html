GARRF Research Note

This Virtual SHM Laboratory uses the smartphone's built-in accelerometer and gyroscope to capture vibration and motion data. The recorded data can be used for preliminary Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), vibration analysis, modal studies, educational demonstrations, and AI-assisted research.

Important: Smartphone sensors are intended for experimental and educational research. They should not be treated as certified structural monitoring instruments without appropriate calibration and validation.
