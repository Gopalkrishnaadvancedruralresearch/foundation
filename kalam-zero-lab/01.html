Experiment 01 — Ohm's Law

Determine and verify the relationship between Voltage, Current and Resistance using a virtual Arduino-based experiment.

The experiment uses a virtual potentiometer as the variable input and displays the experimental values through the Serial Monitor.

V = I × R
Ohm's Law
FIRST EXPERIMENT • GARRF VIRTUAL ENGINEERING LABORATORY

🎯 Objective

  • Study Ohm's Law.
  • Observe the effect of changing voltage on current.
  • Use an Arduino analog input to acquire a variable signal.
  • Calculate current from voltage and known resistance.
  • Verify the relationship experimentally.

📐 Theory

For a resistive load:

V = I × R
I = V / R

The virtual experiment uses a 220 Ω load resistance. The potentiometer provides the variable analog signal.

🧪 Experimental Procedure

TURN THE VIRTUAL POTENTIOMETER
Observe the Voltage, Current and Resistance values.
1 Open the selected virtual laboratory.
2 Start the Wokwi simulation.
3 Turn the virtual potentiometer through its range.
4 Observe the values in the Serial Monitor.
5 Record suitable readings.
6 Calculate current using I = V/R.
7 Compare the calculated and observed values.

For Arduino UNO, Wokwi's Serial Monitor automatically connects to the hardware serial interface when the program prints serial output.

🧠 Engineering Challenge

1. If voltage increases while resistance remains constant, what happens to current?
Current increases proportionally.
2. For V = 5 V and R = 1 kΩ, calculate current.
I = 5/1000 = 0.005 A = 5 mA.
3. What is the purpose of the Arduino analog input?
It acquires the variable analog signal from the potentiometer.
4. What experimental evidence demonstrates Ohm's Law?
The measured/calculated values maintain the relationship V = I × R within the limits of the virtual experiment.
