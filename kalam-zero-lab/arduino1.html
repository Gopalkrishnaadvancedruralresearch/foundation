13 🧪 Student Engineering Challenges

Challenge 1 — Motor Power

Your Arduino robot has two motors. Each motor can draw much more current during startup than during normal running. Should the battery and driver be sized only from the normal running current?

No. Startup/stall current must be considered. A driver and battery that look adequate at normal current can fail or reset during motor startup.
Challenge 2 — Resistor

An LED is connected to a 5 V supply. Its forward voltage is approximately 2 V and the target current is 10 mA. What does the resistor do?

It limits the current through the LED. The resistor converts the excess voltage into electrical power while keeping LED current within the intended range.
Challenge 3 — Battery

If the robot needs the same voltage but twice the operating time, what characteristic of the battery should generally increase?

Battery energy/capacity should generally increase. Voltage should still remain compatible with the robot's power architecture.
Challenge 4 — AI

Your robot can detect an obstacle. What additional information might it need before deciding whether to turn left or right?

It may need additional sensor observations, robot position/orientation, movement state, map information or learned data. This is where robotics begins moving toward intelligent decision-making.
