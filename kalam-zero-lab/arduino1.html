9 🧠 Why Did The System Choose This?

Why can't I connect a motor directly to an Arduino pin?

A microcontroller pin provides a control signal. A motor can require substantially more current and creates electrical disturbances. A suitable motor driver separates the control electronics from the motor power stage.

Why do LEDs need resistors?

The resistor limits current. Without suitable current limiting, the LED or the controlling circuit can be damaged.

Why is battery voltage important?

Every component has an electrical operating range. A battery must provide suitable voltage while also supplying enough current and energy for the required operating time.
