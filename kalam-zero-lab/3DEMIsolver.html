Theoretical Framework & Student Engineering Notes: Annamdas–Soh Coupled 3D EMI Formulation

Coupled 3D PZT-Structure Elastodynamic Constitutive Interaction Tensor • Units shown as $ (Siemens / inverted Ohm $\mho$)

1. Coupled Complex Admittance $Y(\omega)$

The electrical admittance across the bonded piezoceramic patch is governed by electro-mechanical coupling:

Y(ω) = G(ω) + j B(ω)
= j ω (A_p / h_p) · [ ε₃₃ᵀ (1 - j δ) - 2 d₃₁² / (s₁₁ᴱ (1 - j η_p)) · (Z_a / (Z_s + Z_a)) · (tan(k l_p) / (k l_p)) ]

Units are expressed in $ (Siemens / mho), where real part $G$ represents active power dissipation and imaginary part $B$ represents reactive susceptance.

2. 1D vs. 2D vs. 3D Impedance Tensors

  • 1D Slender Beam: Pure unconstrained axial-flexural coupling with scalar mechanical impedance $Z_s(\omega) = \rho A c \tanh(\gamma L)$.
  • 2D Thin Plate: Bi-axial multi-directional planar impedance coupling scaled by $1/(1-\nu)$, showing Chladni 2D nodal grid patterns.
  • 3D Solid Block: Full tri-axial tensorial coupling scaled by $(1-\nu)/((1+\nu)(1-2\nu))$, capturing volumetric shear and Rayleigh wave interactions.

3. Fracture Damage Indexing

The crack introduces localized loss of structural stiffness $(\Delta k_s)$ and damping perturbation $(\Delta c_s)$:

RMSD (%) = √[ Σ (G_damaged - G_healthy)² / Σ (G_healthy)² ] × 100%

Internal core cracks cause volumetric wave scattering, while surface cracks induce dominant Rayleigh surface acoustic wave distortions.
