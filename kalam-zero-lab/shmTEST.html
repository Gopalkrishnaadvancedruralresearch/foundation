VIRUDH — AI Defective Digital Twin & Visual Structural Health Monitoring

Developed under the GARRF research initiative, VIRUDH introduces India's first privately integrated AI-driven Defective Digital Twin framework. Designed to bridge optical baseline comparisons with advanced structural evaluation across a structured two-tier progression model.

VIRUDH Level 1

Surface Visual Inspector

Designed for students and entry-level field inspectors to perform baseline optical verifications:

  • Baseline vs. Current Image Pixel Alignment
  • Automated Visual Delta Extraction
  • Surface Damage Triggers: [Present / Absent]
  • Crack Presence Verification: [Detected / Not Detected]
  • Strict 99% Baseline Confidence Check
VIRUDH Senior

Advanced PZT & NDT Studio

Designed for graduate researchers and senior structural engineers interfacing with shm_pzt.html:

  • Cross-correlation of surface cracking with PZT sensor nodes
  • Electromechanical Impedance (EMI) shift mapping
  • Sub-surface structural defect health tracking
  • Quantitative wave propagation and frequency sweep analysis

Real-time inspection logs and diagnostic feedback from the digital twin instance.
