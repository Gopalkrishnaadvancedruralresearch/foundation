About the Foundation

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation

The GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is an initiative dedicated to promoting research, science, innovation, education and technological development, with a special focus on young students and aspiring researchers.

GARRF seeks to create an environment where young minds are encouraged to ask questions, explore scientific ideas, undertake research, develop innovative solutions and contribute meaningfully to society. The Foundation aims to bridge knowledge, research and practical applications by encouraging interdisciplinary learning and collaboration.

The Foundation places particular emphasis on nurturing scientific curiosity among students and youth, supporting research-oriented learning, encouraging innovation and connecting young researchers with experienced scientists, academics, professionals and international collaborators.

GARRF aspires to develop into a globally respected research organisation that combines scientific excellence with compassion, social responsibility and sustainable development. Its long-term objective is to support affordable and meaningful technologies that can improve lives and contribute to the sustainable development of Bharat.

Dr. Annamdas and His Family

A Foundation Built on Scientific Temperament

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav, a Senior Scientist and Singapore Returnee, with a strong scientific temperament, initiated the GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation with a vision to promote research, science and innovation among youth and young students.

His vision is rooted in the belief that scientific thinking should begin at an early stage and that students should be encouraged not merely to learn existing knowledge, but also to question, investigate, experiment, innovate and create new knowledge.

The Foundation is inspired by family values of education, integrity, service, compassion and respect for knowledge. It seeks to carry these values forward by creating opportunities for young people to explore science, technology, research and innovation.

GARRF therefore represents more than an institutional initiative. It reflects a long-term commitment to encouraging the next generation to develop a scientific outlook, pursue meaningful research and use knowledge for the benefit of society.

Scientific Leadership

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Chief Scientific Officer, GARRF

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav serves as the Chief Scientific Officer of GARRF. He earned his Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, holds a Master of Engineering degree from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, completed a MicroMasters programme through the Rochester Institute of Technology (USA), and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

Over the past two decades, he has been associated with NTU Singapore as a student, researcher, scientist, faculty member, and visiting scholar. His professional journey also includes academic and research experience in Pittsburgh, USA.

His research interests include Structural Health Monitoring, Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twins, Smart Infrastructure, Non-Destructive Evaluation, Sensors, Machine Learning, and Sustainable Engineering.

In recognition of his contributions and achievements, he is an Er. Avinash Shirode "Yashokirti" Awardee 2026 from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru.

His long-term vision is to establish GARRF as a globally respected research organisation that nurtures young innovators, strengthens international collaborations, promotes interdisciplinary research, and develops affordable technologies that improve lives and contribute to the sustainable development of Bharat.

Our Vision

Building a Culture of Scientific Thinking

GARRF envisions a future where young students from diverse backgrounds have the opportunity to discover science, participate in research and develop solutions to real-world problems.

The Foundation seeks to encourage a culture in which curiosity, creativity, critical thinking and evidence- based reasoning are valued and nurtured.

Our Mission

Empowering Young Researchers

Our mission is to promote research and scientific education among youth and young students, provide opportunities for practical learning, encourage innovation and facilitate interaction with experienced scientists, researchers and professionals.

Through its programmes and collaborations, GARRF aims to help young learners transform ideas into research, research into innovation and innovation into meaningful social impact.

Our Values

Principles That Guide GARRF
