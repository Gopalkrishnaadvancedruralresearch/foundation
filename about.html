ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation

The GopalKrishna Advanced Rural Research Foundation (GARRF) is established with a vision of promoting scientific thinking, research, innovation and meaningful education among young people, particularly students and aspiring researchers.

The Foundation seeks to create an environment where curiosity is encouraged, scientific questions are explored, and young minds are given opportunities to learn through research, experimentation, collaboration and real-world problem solving.

GARRF aims to connect students, researchers, scientists, educators and professionals across disciplines and geographies. Through research initiatives, mentoring, educational programmes, scientific discussions and international collaborations, the Foundation seeks to contribute to the development of affordable and sustainable solutions for society.

The Foundation particularly believes that advanced science and technology should not remain limited to large institutions or metropolitan centres. Knowledge, innovation and scientific opportunities should gradually reach rural communities and young students who may have limited access to such resources.

ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

Dr. Annamdas and His Family

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav, a Senior Scientist and Singapore Returnee with a strong scientific temperament, initiated the Foundation with the belief that scientific curiosity and research should be encouraged from a young age.

His vision is to promote research, science, innovation and scientific thinking among youth and young students, while creating opportunities for them to interact with researchers, scientists, educators and professionals.

The Foundation also reflects the encouragement, values and support of his family and elders. GARRF therefore carries forward a broader family vision of education, knowledge, integrity, service and the responsible use of science and technology for the benefit of society.

The Foundation is intended to grow gradually through the participation of students, researchers, mentors, volunteers, collaborators and well-wishers who share the belief that knowledge becomes meaningful when it contributes to society.

SCIENTIFIC LEADERSHIP

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Scientific Leadership • Research • Mentoring • Innovation

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav

Chief Scientific Officer

Dr. Annamdas Venu Gopal Madhav serves as the Chief Scientific Officer of GARRF. He earned his Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, holds a Master of Engineering degree from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, completed a MicroMasters programme through the Rochester Institute of Technology (USA), and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

Over the past two decades, he has been associated with NTU Singapore as a student, researcher, scientist, faculty member and visiting scholar. His professional journey also includes academic and research experience in Pittsburgh, USA.

His research interests include Structural Health Monitoring, Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twins, Smart Infrastructure, Non-Destructive Evaluation, Sensors, Machine Learning and Sustainable Engineering.

A Vision for Young Scientists

His long-term vision is to establish GARRF as a globally respected research organisation that nurtures young innovators, strengthens international collaborations, promotes interdisciplinary research, and develops affordable technologies that improve lives and contribute to the sustainable development of Bharat.

The Foundation aims to provide young students with exposure to scientific thinking, research methodology, emerging technologies and collaborative problem solving, helping them progress from learners to innovators and researchers.
