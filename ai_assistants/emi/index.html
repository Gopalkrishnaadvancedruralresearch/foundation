❓ How to Use the Assistant (Step-by-Step)

  • 1
    Launch the Assistant

    Click the "Launch AI Assistant" button above to open the dedicated GARRF Gem interface in a new window.

  • 2
    Upload Your Signal Data

    Attach or drop your baseline and current state files—accepts screenshots, plot images, PDF reports, Excel files, or CSV exports.

  • 3
    Automatic Axis & Domain Cropping

    If frequency ranges differ (e.g., 10–100 Hz vs. 50–90 Hz), the AI automatically identifies the intersection window and resamples data points.

  • 4
    Review Your Diagnostic Dashboard

    The AI generates an immediate structured analysis including multi-peak tracking, RMSD/MAPD metrics, and structural condition ratings.
